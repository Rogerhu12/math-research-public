## 连续度量界的具体支付

令

\[
U=X_s-\frac{\theta_s}{\rho}X_\psi,
\qquad V=\frac{X_\psi}{\widehat a\rho},\qquad
E=\begin{pmatrix}U\cdot U-1&U\cdot V\\U\cdot V&V\cdot V-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
