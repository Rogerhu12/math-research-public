$$

$$

$$

## 4. Four entire separable nodes and an explicit power dual

Use the fixed order $a=0,1,2,3,4$. A node is accepted for $R$ only when polynomial extended gcd gives

$$
u_a(T)\chi_a'(T)+v_a(T)\chi_a(T)=1.
$$

A cyclic Krylov matrix is not used as a separability claim. A positive-degree gcd is recorded without discarding any factor. If fewer than four entire nodes pass, the consumer stops honestly with its completed stages; it does not compute a new node.

For $0\leq j<272$ let

$$
q_j(T)=\sum_{r=0}^{271-j}c_rT^{271-j-r},\qquad c_0=1.
$$

The power-basis trace-dual column is $q_j/\chi_a'$. Thus, if $Q_a$ has columns $q_j$ and $D_a$ is multiplication by the stored polynomial inverse $u_a\bmod\chi_a$,

$$
H_a^{-1}=D_aQ_a,\qquad
R(a)=M_g(a)K_aD_aQ_aK_a^t.
$$

The multiplication matrix $D_a$ is made by repeated multiplication by $T$ modulo the entire degree-272 polynomial. No dense matrix inverse is called. Both complete products $H_a(D_aQ_a)$ and $(D_aQ_a)H_a$ are checked, followed by the full $R(a)G(a)=M_g(a)$ and symmetry identities.

The first four accepted nodes determine each entry of $R$ with degree at most three. All entries exceeding $3-\epsilon_i-\epsilon_j$ must be zero. In particular the entire $127\times127$ bottom block is zero. All four complete node matrices are multiplied back.
