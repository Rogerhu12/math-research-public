`83a18d8a4236567bbd3b0f76920f37de1ded1085b33bf4275507c039922125f2`.
The literal source plan is
`notes/R901-original-five-Z-shared-flip-source-plan.pending.json`,
47,169 bytes, SHA-256
`7d5300ec14bf83119768d6f41d44e940e5e073348d765538f154f51ddf69431a`.

The plan is immutable mathematical input. Its null future-source slots and zero
actual-output counters describe its status when written. They are not rewritten

## 7. Atomic stages, compaction and memory

All new mathematical stages are root-directory
`R901-*.json.gz` files with `complete=true` and one exact new configuration.
The mutable `R901-current.json.gz` has `complete=false`.

The R894 compact product stage saves the selected original product-column
identities, the entire 1,904 by 962 basis and its selected inverse. It copies
already computed data; it does not run a second product elimination. The full
membership matrix, complete 146 kernel, successful full pencil relation,
418 projection, unscaled anchor, and principal remain live.

Only after a complete flip certificate and an atomic closed-work receipt exist
may non-live scan stages be removed. Their complete byte counts and SHA-256
values become historical records, never live file references. Active and pending
work is not removed. The live source graph includes all necessary parent
unit-solve definitions.

The in-memory matrices and fibre caches of a node are released after its last
future parent use. This deletes no mathematical file and changes no exact
principal root. A disk floor or time limit returns a resumable incomplete
endpoint.

## 8. Resume and honest endpoints

Resume imports only same-core, same-source, same-plan, same-method stages.
The selected source carrier and its inventory/receipt are fully checked before
arithmetic. All present new complete stages and the actual current checkpoint
are preserved. A previous false final certificate is historical and does not
constrain a later current checkpoint.

Versioned stage inventories from earlier incomplete endpoints may legitimately
name scan files that a later complete flip has compacted. Only the current
successful final inventory is asserted to equal the present live mathematical
inventory. Historical inventories retain their original bytes; their status is
explicit in the IO verification.

The final certificate distinguishes actual completed new flips, the two reused
flips, and actually completed directions. All five direction flags can become
true only after the five full terminal marked outputs exist. A finite
`maximum_new_flips` prefix, a search-prefix stop, budget or disk stop, or an
unexpected exception does not become `complete=true`.
