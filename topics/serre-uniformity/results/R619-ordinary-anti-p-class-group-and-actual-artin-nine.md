# R619: the ordinary anti-p class group and the actual auxiliary Artin value 9

**Status: proved.** In the original p=43, q and Weil marking, let
F_t/F be the pure anti-p extension and put N=L F_t. Then the ordinary
character class group satisfies

\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Cl}(N)_{\chi^-}=k,
 \quad \Gamma\text{ and }T\text{ act trivially},
 \quad N_T:\operatorname{Cl}(N)_{\chi^-}
       \overset\sim\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}(L)_{\chi^-}.}
 \tag{619.1}
\]

Extension of ideals in the other direction is zero. The actual
anti-p Robert/Hilbert 90 construction below has the coefficient

\[
 \boxed{C_t=9\in\mathbf F_{43}\subset k.}             \tag{619.2}
\]

This is the auxiliary anti-p coefficient. It is **not** the original
scalar coefficient C, the original Norm first moment J, the ordinary
coefficient b, or the original scalar \(\mathcal L\). Their values remain
unknown. No old, lost ordinary unit lattice or assertion
\(A'=R_\Gamma/\pi^{43}\) is used.

## 1. Fields, ordinary modules and exact inputs

Use the same fields and marked generators as R561/R613:

\[

 F=K(A[p])^{\mathbf F_p^\times},\quad A:y^2=x^3-x,
\]
\[
 L=F L_q,\quad \Gamma=\langle\gamma\rangle=\operatorname{Gal}(L/F),
 \quad B=K(A[p^2])^{\mu_{42}},
\]
\[
 S=\langle s=1+p\rangle,\quad T=\langle t=1+21pi\rangle,
 \quad F_t=B^S,\quad N=L F_t.
\]

Here \(\operatorname{Gal}(B/F)=S\times T\), and
\(\kappa_p(t)=1\). The symbol N without a subscript is the field;
\(N_\Gamma,N_T,N_S\) denote norm operators. The prime q is the original
776-bit prime of R171. The extension F_t/F ramifies only at p and is
completely split at q. The extension L/F is totally ramified at every
q prime and split above p and 2. Thus N/F_t is a cyclic Gamma extension
totally ramified at q, and N/L is a cyclic T extension split at q.

Let \(\Delta=\langle\sigma\rangle=C_{44}\), where sigma is the
actual primary beta direction \(\beta=-1+2i\), with q component 1.
Put

\[
 \mathcal O=\mathbf Z_{43}[i],\quad k=\mathcal O/43,
 \quad P_+=\sum_{j=0}^{43}\widehat\omega^j\sigma^j,
 \quad \omega=8+25i.
\]

The Teichmuller lift is the same one as R561. The operator is
\(44e_{\chi^-}\), **without division by 44**. Its field and divisor
eigencharacter is \(\widehat\omega^{-1}\).

For a number field V in this diagram, write

\[
 E_V=(\mathcal O_V^\times\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-},\quad
 D_V=(\operatorname{Div}_{\rm fin}V\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-},
 \quad A_V=(\operatorname{Cl}(V)\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-}.
\]

These are **ordinary** units, full finite divisors and ordinary ideal
classes. Let \(P_V\subset D_V\) be the principal divisor submodule.
The finite class modules mean their p-primary parts. Multiplicative
groups are written additively after tensoring and taking this summand.
Flatness and the prime-to-p integral idempotent preserve all the exact
sequences used below.

The published inputs are:

The logarithm in (619.3) is the original power-residue logarithm

## 2. The complete ordinary unit and class modules of F_t

First we recover these inputs directly, so no lost full-level unit
lattice is needed:

\[
 E_{F_t}=\mathcal O[T]u_t,\qquad A_{F_t}=0.          \tag{619.4}
\]

The Dirichlet logarithmic representation, in the nontrivial Delta
character, is the regular T representation. Thus
\(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathcal O}E_{F_t}=p\).
The field contains no \(\mu_p\): its absolute degree \(88p\) is not
divisible by \(p-1=42\). Hence this lattice is torsion free, and
\(E_{F_t}^T=E_F=\mathcal O u\).

The element u is not in \(pE_{F_t}\). If u=pv, torsion freeness would
make v T-invariant, contrary to the primitivity of u in E_F.
Modulo p, put x=t-1. Then
\(k[T]=k[x]/(x^p)\) and \(N_T=x^{p-1}\). Since
\(N_T\bar u_t=\bar u\ne0\), the cyclic submodule generated by
\(\bar u_t\) has dimension p. It is the entire unit module modulo p.
The map \(\mathcal O[T]\to E_{F_t}\) is between equal-rank free
\(\mathcal O\)-modules and has determinant a unit. This proves the
integral, saturated unit assertion in (619.4).

For the class assertion, at p the whole nontrivial Delta character
is inertial, so the p-prime divisor module has no \(\chi^-\) summand.
At all other finite primes F_t/F is unramified. Therefore every
T-invariant character divisor is the extension of a divisor of F:

\[
 D_{F_t}^T=jD_F.                                   \tag{619.5}
\]

Hilbert 90 and
\(0\to E_{F_t}\to(F_t^\times\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-}
\to P_{F_t}\to0\) give an injection
\(H^1(T,P_{F_t})\hookrightarrow H^2(T,E_{F_t})=0\).
Taking invariants in \(0\to P_{F_t}\to D_{F_t}\to A_{F_t}\to0\)
now shows that \(A_{F_t}^T\) is generated by invariant divisors.
By (619.5) and \(A_F=0\), this invariant class module is zero.
A nonzero finite p-primary T module has nonzero invariants: first take
its nonzero p-torsion and then use the nilpotence of t-1. Therefore
\(A_{F_t}=0\), proving (619.4).

## 3. The actual local Norm obstruction in N/F_t

Fix the marked q prime \(P_0\) of F_t. It is the unique branch fixed
by the original tau. The q primes of F_t over the marked prime of F
are its T translates. Put

\[
 A_\ell(T)=\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}\ell_{P_0}(t^ju_t)T^{-j}
             \in k[T]/(T^p-1).                     \tag{619.6}
\]

This convention makes reduction of tX equal to T times reduction of X.
Equations (619.3) and \(N_Tu_t=u\) give exactly

\[
 A_\ell(1)=0,\qquad A_\ell'(1)=-13i=30i\ne0.       \tag{619.7}
\]

Consequently, in the local ring \(k[T]=k[T-1]/(T-1)^p\),

\[
 (A_\ell(T))=(T-1),\qquad
 \dim_k(A_\ell(T))=p-1=42.                        \tag{619.8}
\]

This conclusion uses the actual first derivative, not merely the
vanishing of the augmentation.

At q, N/F_t is cyclic tame totally ramified of degree p. A unit is a
local norm precisely when its residue is a pth power. After Delta
projection, the direct sum of these local obstruction groups is one
copy of k[T]. By the complete unit lattice (619.4), the image of the
global unit module is precisely the ideal in (619.8), up to the
irrelevant invertible scalar in the reciprocity convention.

At p and 2 the Gamma extension is split. At every other finite prime
it is unramified, where local units are norms. There are no real
places. The cyclic Hasse Norm theorem therefore gives

\[
 d:=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O}
   \frac{E_{F_t}}{E_{F_t}\cap N_\Gamma(N^\times\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-}}
   =42.                                            \tag{619.9}
\]

The use of the theorem after completion is exact: its usual injective
map from the global norm cokernel to the direct sum of the local
cokernels is tensored with the flat ring \(\mathcal O\), then projected
to the Delta direct summand. All these cokernels are killed by p.

## 4. The full ambiguous-class calculation

We give the ordinary divisor exact sequences, including the unit
indices which would otherwise be easy to omit.

The only ramified character divisors in N/F_t are the q primes.
There are p T translates after taking the Delta summand, so

\[
 D_N^\Gamma/jD_{F_t}\simeq k[T].                  \tag{619.10}
\]

All other finite divisors descend with ramification index one; no
outside support is thrown away. Since \(A_{F_t}=0\), the invariant
principal-divisor sequence and Hilbert 90 inject
\(H^1(\Gamma,E_N)\) into (619.10). If \(A_N^{\rm st}\) is the image
of \(D_N^\Gamma\) in \(A_N\), then

\[
 0\longrightarrow H^1(\Gamma,E_N)
 \longrightarrow k[T]\longrightarrow A_N^{\rm st}
 \longrightarrow0.                               \tag{619.11}
\]

The full divisor module is a sum of permutation modules; its first
cohomology is zero (for each stabilizer, a homomorphism from a finite
group to the torsion-free additive module is zero). Thus

\[
 0\longrightarrow A_N^{\rm st}\longrightarrow A_N^\Gamma
 \longrightarrow H^1(\Gamma,P_N)\longrightarrow0, \tag{619.12}
\]

where the multiplicative exact sequence identifies the last group as

\[
 H^1(\Gamma,P_N)=
 \frac{E_{F_t}\cap N_\Gamma(N^\times\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-}}
      {N_\Gamma E_N}.                             \tag{619.13}
\]

Write \(h_j=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O}H^j(\Gamma,E_N)\)
for j=1,2. The Dirichlet representation gives
\(E_N\otimes\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O)\) as p copies of the
regular Gamma representation. It follows that the Herbrand quotient
of E_N is 1, or

\[
 h_1=h_2.                                         \tag{619.14}
\]

To justify this integrally, choose a Gamma-stable lattice commensurable
with \(\mathcal O[\Gamma]^p\). Regular lattices have quotient 1,
and every finite Gamma module has equal cardinalities in its two
Tate cohomology groups. Passing across finite-index inclusions does
not change the quotient. This argument uses the nontrivial Delta
summand; no missing trivial-character factor of p is inserted.

Since \(H^2(\Gamma,E_N)=E_{F_t}/N_\Gamma E_N\), (619.9) and
(619.13) give
\(\operatorname{length}H^1(\Gamma,P_N)=h_2-d\).
Combining (619.11)--(619.14), the two unknown unit-cohomology indices
cancel:

\[
 \operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O}A_N^\Gamma
 =p-h_1+(h_2-d)=43-42=1.                           \tag{619.15}
\]

In particular this does not assume that every globally normic unit is
already the norm of an ordinary unit.

## 5. The actual genus Artin value forces the exponent to be one

Let

\[
 R_\Gamma=\mathcal O[\Gamma]/(N_\Gamma)
          =\mathcal O[\zeta_p],\qquad \pi=\gamma-1.
\]

This is a discrete valuation ring, since \(\Phi_p(1+X)\) is
Eisenstein over the unramified ring \(\mathcal O\). The class norm to
F_t is zero by (619.4). Therefore the group-ring norm acts as zero on
\(A_N\), and \(A_N\) is a finite torsion R_Gamma module.
Its Gamma invariants are its pi-annihilated socle. Equation (619.15)
and the elementary divisor theorem consequently give

\[
 A_N\simeq R_\Gamma/\pi^d\quad\text{for some }d\ge1. \tag{619.16}
\]

One must still rule out d>1; a nonzero ambiguous class alone would
not do so. We use its **actual nonzero image in a quotient killed by
pi**, as follows.

Let H/F be the strict-p genus field used and normalized in R561. It
is the elementary abelian q-ray extension in the two-dimensional
Delta type, of order \(p^2\) over F. It is split at p and 2 and
unramified elsewhere except q. Its \(\chi^-\) coordinate is the
one in which \(F_0\mapsto1\) and \(K_0\mapsto-i\), and the positive
q Frobenius has value 32.

The intersection \(H\cap N=F\), because Delta acts trivially on
\(\operatorname{Gal}(N/F)=\Gamma\times T\), whereas the nonzero
Delta type of H has no trivial quotient. The compositum HN/N is
unramified. At q, HN/F is abelian of exponent p. Its tame inertia
is cyclic and hence has order at most p; N/F already has inertia p,
so the relative inertia above N is trivial. At p and 2, H was split;
at the other places it was unramified. This checks every finite
place and does not apply the exponent-p argument to a nonabelian
extension.

Hilbert class theory supplies the surjective character map

\[
 \varphi:A_N\longrightarrow k
\]

from HN/N. Both Gamma and T act trivially on this quotient, because
HN/F is abelian. Thus \(\varphi\) factors through \(A_N/\pi A_N\).

Let \(Q_N\) be the marked q prime above \(Q_L\), and put
\(e_N=[P_+Q_N]\). Gamma fixes this prime, so \(e_N\) is in the
socle. Its residue degree over F is one: the Gamma extension is
totally ramified and the T extension split. Compatibility of positive
Artin with base change and the exact R561 normalization gives

\[
 \varphi(e_N)=44\cdot32\ne0\quad\text{in }k.       \tag{619.17}
\]

This is the promised nonzero image modulo pi, not an inference from
the mere nonvanishing of e_N in the original class group. If d>1 in
(619.16), every socle element belongs to \(\pi A_N\) and hence maps
to zero under \(\varphi\). Equation (619.17) rules this out. Therefore
d=1. The same map is now an isomorphism and shows that T also acts
trivially. The class e_N is a k-basis.

The norm \(N_T:A_N\to A_L\) maps e_N to e_q, since the chosen q
prime has residue degree one over L. Both are nonzero k-bases, so
this norm is an isomorphism. Extension of e_q to N is the sum of
all p T translates of e_N. They are equal in A_N and pA_N=0; hence
this extension is zero. This proves all assertions in (619.1).

## 6. The particular Robert/Hilbert 90 class

We now evaluate a specified principalization class, rather than just
prove that some class capitulates.

R613 constructs one ancestor ordinary unit \(u_B\in B\) and one
actual mixed p-squared-q product \(c_B\in LB\), with the original
q-inverse-p-point convention, satisfying

\[
 N_Su_B=u_t,\quad N_Tu_t=u,\quad
 N_{S\times T}c_B=c_q,\quad
 N_\Gamma c_B=(1-s^{11})u_B.                       \tag{619.18}
\]

These are exact distribution identities for
\(v(Q)=64^{10}/\psi_{11,A}(Q)\), based at the same
\(P_2=(i,1-i)\) and real p-squared point R. In particular they do
not combine unrelated norm witnesses. Define

\[
 c_t=N_Sc_B\in N.
\]

The commuting norms give

\[
 N_Tc_t=c_q,\qquad N_\Gamma c_t=0.                \tag{619.19}
\]

The second identity is exact because
\(N_S(1-s^{11})=0\). Keep the same original R561 ordinary norm unit
y and balanced derivative:

\[
 N_\Gamma y=u,\quad py=u+aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}c_q,
 \quad a=15i/7,
 \quad D_\Gamma^{\rm bal}=\sum_{j=-21}^{21}j\gamma^j.
\]

Set

\[
 v_t=u_t-y+aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}c_t.
\]

Then \(N_Tv_t=u-py+aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}c_q=0\).
Hilbert 90 supplies a completed ordinary multiplicative element z_t
such that

\[
 (t-1)z_t=v_t.                                    \tag{619.20}
\]

The right side is an ordinary unit. Thus \(\operatorname{div}z_t\)
is T-invariant. The only ramification of N/L is at p, whose divisor
module has zero \(\chi^-\) part because Delta is inertial. Every
other finite prime is unramified. There is a full divisor D_t of L
with

\[
 \operatorname{div}_N z_t=j_{N/L}D_t.              \tag{619.21}
\]

Every finite outside-support term remains in D_t. Changing the
Hilbert 90 solution changes D_t by a principal divisor of L; hence
\([D_t]\) is a well-defined ordinary class. It lies in the ordinary
capitulation kernel, and we define C_t by

\[
 [D_t]=C_te_q.                                    \tag{619.22}
\]

Since \(N_\Gamma c_t=0\),
\(N_\Gamma v_t=pu_t-u=(t-1)D_Tu_t\). Therefore

\[
 h_t=N_\Gamma z_t-D_Tu_t\in(F^\times\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-},
 \qquad \operatorname{div}_Fh_t=N_\Gamma D_t.       \tag{619.23}
\]

This is the same full principal norm required for the ordinary Artin
formula in R561. Its divisor equality follows from norm compatibility
and injectivity of extension on divisor groups, not from discarding
primes away from q.

## 7. Its actual q phase is minus 13i

At the marked q place of N, T is split. Thus the completion is just
the totally ramified Gamma extension of \(K_q=\mathbf Q_{q^2}\).
Its residue field is \(\mathbf F_{q^2}\), and
\(v_p(q^2-1)=1\): the residue p-primary group is exactly \(\mu_p\),
not \(\mu_{p^2}\).

Use the same real R207 q-ray uniformizer theta to remove the valuation
of z_t. The p-primary phase of \(N_\Gamma\theta/q\) is zero: this
is a rational q-unit in the marked real local field, and
\(p\nmid q-1\). For the remaining unit part, Gamma acts trivially
on residue, so its norm raises the residue to the pth power. This
kills its entire p-primary part. Linearity gives, after removing any
q valuation,

\[
 \ell(N_\Gamma z_t)=0.
\]

The unit term in (619.23) is the exact original anti-p derivative,
so (619.3) yields

\[
 \boxed{\ell(h_t)=-\ell(D_Tu_t)=-13i.}             \tag{619.24}
\]

No choice of a local root has been substituted for the global Hilbert
90 solution. Here the phase vanishing follows for every solution from
the actual local residue degree and the fixed real uniformizer.

The full ordinary genus Artin calculation, with the convention of
R561, is

\[
 \operatorname{Artin}(D_t)=-44i\ell(h_t),\qquad
 \operatorname{Artin}(P_+Q_L)=44\cdot32.             \tag{619.25}
\]

For clarity, the first formula uses the entire principal divisor in
(619.23). Global reciprocity makes the Artin sum away from q the
negative of the full q-local sum. Adding the q-valuation terms of
D_t cancels the Frobenius terms \(32v_q(h_t)\). The transported
Delta weights leave 44 equal unit contributions. The positive local
unit value is \(+i\ell\), so the remaining global sign is minus.
Consequently no omitted outside divisor can alter (619.25).

Divide the two formulas in (619.25) and use (619.24):

\[
 C_t=-\frac{i}{32}(-13i)=-\frac{13}{32}=9\pmod{43}.
\]

This proves (619.2) and identifies the actual class (619.22), with its
sign, field coefficient and original Weil marking. Its nonzero value
also directly witnesses the capitulation in (619.1).

## 8. Boundary of the conclusion and small arithmetic check

The number 9 is a real coefficient in k. The original scalar class C
in R561 is purely imaginary; no equality between them is asserted.
R613 gives \(C_r=C+34r\), and the numerical identity \(34=-9\)
is consistent with the auxiliary anti-p direction. It does not
determine the unknown value at r=0.

The new argument does not establish a full ordinary unit lattice for
N, the length of the full ordinary class module of LB, or its T action.
In particular it does not fill the unproved mixed-character
nonvanishing step of the working candidate unit lattice. Nor does it
evaluate an element X_t with
\(\operatorname{div}X_t=P_+P_0\), \(N_TX_t=P_+\alpha_F\), or its
R599 residue first moment J.

The small standard-library checker recomputes the weighted base and
first-derivative values from R198's actual 44-row certificate. It
checks the power-log convention, the norm-index lengths, the exact
Artin quotient and its nonzero value. It does not rerun the large
Robert evaluations or pretend that a finite calculation proves the
class-field exact sequences of sections 2--7.
