# R780: the exact ordinary T norm image and the automatic deep T condition

Date: 2026-11-14. **Status: proved.** Assume $b\ne0$ in the
original $p=43$ problem. The complete ordinary T norm image is

$$
\boxed{
N_TE_M=\mathcal O[\Gamma,S]c_s+\mathcal O[S]u_s
\subset E_V,\qquad V=M^T.
}
\tag{780.1}
$$

Its quotient in $E_V$ is k, generated by the original unit y.
Moreover the entire specified invariant space from R773 satisfies

$$
\boxed{
ku\oplus kn\ \subset\
xzX^{42}(E_M/pE_M),
\qquad x=\gamma-1,\ z=s-1,\ X=t-1.
}
\tag{780.2}
$$

The preimages in (780.2) can be represented by actual ordinary
units with all three norms zero. Thus neither membership in
the true T norm image nor the mod-p depth condition
$xzX^{41}(E_M/pE_M)$ from R766 can determine the remaining
coefficient $\eta$ in $U_V=(4/C)n+\eta u$.

The assertion is about these specified membership conditions.
It does not replace the original q-principalization, original
first moment, or full cochain equality by a convenient new
witness. No value of $\eta_I,b,J_\ell,\sigma_2$ is inferred.

## 1. The actual class transfer and ordinary capitulation

Keep the original fields, markings, coefficient ring
$\mathcal O=\mathbf Z_{43}[i]$, and $k=\mathcal O/p$.
R715 proves the actual ordinary endpoint

$$
A_M=R_\Gamma/\pi^{43},\qquad \pi=\gamma-1.
$$

When $b\ne0$, the action of $t-1$ has first term
$b\pi$ with b in $\mathbf F_{43}^{\times}$.
The full ordinary descent and transfer of R684 therefore give

$$
A_V=A_M/(t-1)A_M=k,\qquad j_{M/V}=0.
\tag{780.3}
$$

To retain the transfer coefficient, in the original cyclic
ring one has

$$
N_T\equiv(b^{42}-1)\pi^{42}\pmod{\pi^{43}}.
$$

The first term p of the norm polynomial is
$-\pi^{42}$ modulo $\pi^{43}$; its last term contributes
$b^{42}\pi^{42}$ and all intermediate terms vanish at
this precision. As $b^{42}=1$, the transfer is zero.
This uses R684's already proved identification of the
ordinary transfer, including its full-divisor terms.

For the T extension $M/V$, Hilbert 90 and the full ordinary
divisor sequence identify

$$
H^1(T,E_M)
\simeq\ker(A_V\longrightarrow A_M).
\tag{780.4}
$$

There is no extra ramified divisor term in (780.4).
At p, where T ramifies, the original nontrivial tame
character kills the prime-divisor module. The 2-divisor
module vanishes in the same character. The extension
is split at q in the original marking and is unramified
at every remaining finite prime. Invariant character
divisors therefore descend integrally, exactly as in
the ordinary capitulation arguments of R561/R715.

For clarity, the map sends the ordinary cocycle
$(t-1)h$ to the descended divisor class of h.
If that class is zero, h differs from an element of V
by an ordinary unit, so the cocycle is an ordinary
coboundary. Conversely an actual capitulating divisor
has a principalization h giving this cocycle.
Equations (780.3)--(780.4) prove

$$
\boxed{H^1(T,E_M)=k.}
\tag{780.5}
$$

## 2. The unit Herbrand quotient and exact norm index

The rational T representation on the ordinary character
units is regular, with multiplicity $p^2$:

$$
E_M\otimes_{\mathcal O}\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O)
\simeq\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O)[T]^{p^2}.
\tag{780.6}
$$

This is the original nontrivial character part of the
Dirichlet unit representation. Over the imaginary
quadratic base K, the archimedean permutation
representation is regular; the one subtracted trivial
line has zero component in this nontrivial Delta
character. The Gamma and S indices give the indicated
multiplicity.

Thus $E_M$ is commensurable with $\mathcal O[T]^{p^2}$
as a T lattice. The Herbrand quotient of the latter
is one, since both Tate groups vanish. Passing through
a finite-index common lattice does not change it:
for any finite cyclic T module, the two finite Tate
groups have the same order, by the kernel--image
count for $t-1$ and $N_T$.
Consequently

$$
\frac{|\widehat H^0(T,E_M)|}{|H^1(T,E_M)|}=1.
$$

The fixed ordinary lattice is exactly
$E_M^T=E_V$. There are no missing p-power roots of
unity, and taking invariants preserves integrality
of ordinary units. Therefore (780.5) gives the
complete ordinary norm index

$$
\boxed{[E_V:N_TE_M]=|k|=43^2.}
\tag{780.7}
$$

This is an ordinary unit norm index. It is not
obtained by replacing ordinary unit norms with
norms of arbitrary field elements.

## 3. The original presentation identifies the whole norm image

Put

$$
Z_T=\mathcal O[\Gamma,S]c_s+\mathcal O[S]u_s.
$$

The original ancestors satisfy $N_Tc_B=c_s$ and
$N_Tu_B=u_s$. All operators commute, so

$$
Z_T\subset N_TE_M.
\tag{780.8}
$$

R773's complete integral presentation, valid for
$b\ne0$, gives

$$
E_V=Z_T+\mathcal O[\Gamma]y,\qquad
sy=y,
$$
$$
py=u+aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}c_q,\qquad
(\gamma-1)y=a\gamma^{22}c_q,\qquad
c_q=N_Sc_s,\quad u=N_Su_s.
\tag{780.9}
$$

Thus p and $\gamma-1$ kill the class of y in
$E_V/Z_T$, and the quotient is at most a copy of k.
It is exactly k: in the original presentation of
R773, send c and every $s^ju_s$ to zero and send y
to $1\in k$. Its sole integral relation
$pY-N_SU_s-aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}N_Sc=0$
maps to zero. The Gamma and S action formulas
also respect this map. Hence it is a well-defined
surjection $E_V\to k$ with $Z_T$ in the kernel.

It follows that

$$
E_V/Z_T=k,
\tag{780.10}
$$

with Gamma and S acting trivially on the quotient.
The inclusions (780.8) and the equal indices
(780.7), (780.10) prove (780.1).

In particular y itself is not an ordinary T norm
when $b\ne0$, whereas $py$, $(\gamma-1)y$, $c_s$,
$u_s$, and u are ordinary T norms.

## 4. Explicit preimages for the entire specified invariant space

Work now modulo p, with

$$
x=\gamma-1,\qquad z=s-1,\qquad X=t-1,\qquad c=c_s.
$$

The original relation $c_s=N_Tc_B$ gives

$$
c=X^{42}c_B\quad\text{in }E_M/pE_M.
\tag{780.11}
$$

This is the actual mod-p group-ring norm identity;
it does not identify an arbitrary cyclic module
with the original T direction.

R773 specifies

$$
n=x^{40}z^{42}c+i x^{41}z^{41}c-x^{42}z^{40}c,
\qquad
u=-4x^{42}z^{41}c.
\tag{780.12}
$$

The latter relation also follows directly from
$N_\Gamma c=(1-s^{11})u_s$ and $N_Su_s=u$,
as in R779, and retains the original coefficient
$1/11=4$.

Define actual ordinary unit expressions in $E_M$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_n&=X^{42}
 (x^{39}z^{41}+i x^{40}z^{40}-x^{41}z^{39})c_B,\\
L_u&=-4X^{42}x^{41}z^{40}c_B.
\end{aligned}
\tag{780.13}
$$

All coefficients are integral in $\mathcal O$.
Equations (780.11)--(780.12) give

$$
\boxed{xzL_n\equiv n,\qquad xzL_u\equiv u\pmod{pE_M}.}
\tag{780.14}
$$

Each expression in (780.13) contains a positive
augmentation power for Gamma, S, and T.
The exact group-ring identities $N_\Gamma x=0$,
$N_Sz=0$, and $N_TX=0$ therefore prove, integrally,

$$
N_\Gamma L_n=N_SL_n=N_TL_n=0,
$$
$$
N_\Gamma L_u=N_SL_u=N_TL_u=0.
\tag{780.15}
$$

For any $\lambda,\eta\in k$, take integral coefficient
lifts and set $L_{\lambda,\eta}=\lambda L_n+\eta L_u$.
It is an ordinary unit in $X^{42}E_M$, has all three
norms zero, and satisfies

$$
xzL_{\lambda,\eta}\equiv\lambda n+\eta u\pmod{pE_M}.
\tag{780.16}
$$

This proves the whole inclusion (780.2) with actual
ordinary preimages. It also proves that every vector
in that span lies in the T norm image: this follows
already from (780.1), since n and u are combinations
of $c_s,u_s$.

## 5. Exact consequence for the attempted further restriction

For the actual original target, R773 gives

$$
U_V=\frac4C n+\eta u,\qquad C=9ib\ne0.
$$

Equation (780.1) shows that this entire family belongs
to the complete ordinary T norm image, for every eta.
Equation (780.16) gives an ordinary preimage with
three zero norms and T depth 42 for every eta.
Thus the weaker mod-p depth condition
$xzX^{41}(E_M/pE_M)$ is satisfied automatically as well.

These conclusions do not assert that
$L_{\lambda,\eta}$ equals R766's actual common
primitive, or that its q phases realize the
original first moment. They show exactly why
the indicated norm-image and depth memberships
alone cannot supply the missing scalar:
their solution set already contains the entire
specified two-dimensional invariant space.

Any further use of the original cube must retain
additional actual cochain data or marked local
values. The necessary arithmetic input in R779
remains the p-local value of the specified mixed
Robert expression. Its coordinates, the original b,
and the original $J_\ell$ are still uncomputed.

When $b=0$, the class transfer and full scalar-lattice
generation used in §§1--3 have a different boundary.
The norm-image equality (780.1) and its index are not
asserted there. The mod-p identities in §4 use only
the original norm formulas, but they are not used
to extend the conditional full-lattice statement.
