# R783: a canonical quadratic local coefficient for the original ordinary obstruction

Date: 2026-11-15. **Status: proved reduction and proved local ambiguity.**
The two coefficients specified below have not been numerically evaluated.
All original Robert, tame, Gamma, scalar, real, and unaveraged-character
markings remain unchanged.

Let $x=\gamma-1$ and $z=s-1$. After quotienting the actual semilocal
unit module by the known pure-$p$ ordinary units, the original mixed
Robert unit has a canonical quadratic expansion

$$
\overline c_s=A x^2+B xz+O((x,z)^3).
$$

The coordinate generator and the precise meaning of this expansion
are fixed in §§1–3. Its actual coefficient

$$
\Theta=B-iA
$$

satisfies, for every value of the original $b$, including zero,

$$
\boxed{
\Theta=13b,\qquad
b=10\Theta,\qquad
\Omega_2/\varepsilon=22-2\Theta.
}
\tag{783.1}
$$

In particular the original possible zero obstruction is exactly
$\Theta=11$. These are identities in $\mathbf F_{43}$;
$\Theta$ has not been assigned a numerical value.

The coefficient is independent of the auxiliary local norm preimage
used to write the expansion. However, the original Robert unit cannot
be replaced by an arbitrary local solution of its two Euler norm
equations: an explicit real norm-preserving perturbation can change
its descended class by every vector in the original two-dimensional
local Kummer space. This does not produce new global ordinary units.

## 1. Actual integral local modules and the fixed output normalization

Retain R779's
$p=43$, $\mathcal O=\mathbf Z_{43}[i]$, $k=\mathcal O/p$,
$\Gamma=\langle\gamma\rangle$, $S=\langle s\rangle$,
$F_s$, $L_q$, and $V=F_sL_q$. Write $U_F$ and $U_{F_s}$
for the actual principal local unit modules in the original
$\chi^-$ component, and $U$ for the corresponding semilocal
unit module of $V$ at all primes above $p$.
All multiplicative groups are written additively after completion
and the original unaveraged $P_+$ projection.

The fixed pure-$p$ unit is $u$, its original scalar ancestor is $u_s$,
and the actual local element
$w_q=P_+[\alpha_F]$, with $\alpha_F=1+89460y_F$,
is the restriction of R779's $A_\alpha$.
In particular its Kummer class is the original $d_q$.
The exact normalizations are

$$
N_Su_s=u,\qquad
[d_0]|_F=(i/1848)[u],\qquad
[d_q]|_F=[w_q].
\tag{783.2}
$$

The original R185 basis gives
$(d_0)_p=(24,20)$ and $(d_q)_p=(20,29)$.
Their determinant is nonzero modulo $43$.
Since $1848$ and $i$ are units, $u,w_q$ therefore form an
$\mathcal O$-basis of $U_F$ by Nakayama's lemma, using
R327's rank-two local-unit lattice.

R701 §2 proves $N_SU_{F_s}=U_F$ and
$U_{F_s}\simeq\mathcal O[S]^2$.
Choose any actual $v_q\in U_{F_s}$ such that

$$N_Sv_q=w_q.$$

Then $u_s,v_q$ is an $\mathcal O[S]$-basis.
Here is the exact integral argument. The map from the free
rank-two group-ring module defined by those two columns has
independent norm images $u,w_q$ modulo $p$ in the upper unit
lattice. The base lattice embeds $p$-saturatedly: if $ph$ is
$S$-fixed in the torsion-free upper lattice, then $h$ is
$S$-fixed. Every nonzero submodule of $k[S]^2$ meets its
socle, generated by $N_S$. Independence on that socle makes
the map modulo $p$ injective, hence bijective by equal
dimension. Nakayama then gives the integral assertion.

Fix the same zeroth $p$-adic branch of $L_q$ as R704, and
order the other branches by the same positive $\gamma$.
Since $p$ splits in $L_q$,

$$
U\simeq\mathcal O[\Gamma]\otimes_{\mathcal O}U_{F_s}
       \simeq\mathcal O[\Gamma,S]^2.
\tag{783.3}
$$

Put $u_s$ and $v_q$, respectively, in the zeroth component and
zero in all other components; call these two vectors $e_u,e_q$.
In additive completed-unit notation, zero means the identity unit.
Then

$$
N_\Gamma e_u=u_s,\qquad N_\Gamma e_q=v_q,\qquad
N_\Gamma N_Se_u=u,\qquad N_\Gamma N_Se_q=w_q,
\tag{783.4}
$$

where the right-hand sides are diagonally embedded.

The quotient

$$
\mathcal Q
=U/\mathcal O[\Gamma,S]e_u
\simeq\mathcal O[\Gamma,S]e_q
\tag{783.5}
$$

is thus an actual local quotient by the known pure-$p$ ordinary
unit lattice in every Gamma branch. It is not a quotient by
an unknown global unit lattice in $V$.

## 2. Why the actual original class has no constant or linear term

Let $c=c_s$ be exactly R613/R773's mixed Robert unit, including
the original $s^{11}$ shift.
In $\mathcal Q/p\mathcal Q$, write its image uniquely as

$$
\overline c=f(x,z)e_q,\qquad
f\in R:=k[x,z]/(x^{43},z^{43}).
\tag{783.6}
$$

This is an additive coordinate expression in the completed
multiplicative module; it is not an additive representation
of the raw algebraic unit as a field element.

The actual Euler norm equation

$$N_\Gamma c=(1-s^{11})u_s$$

has zero image in $\mathcal Q$. Since
$N_\Gamma=x^{42}$ in $R$,

$$x^{42}f=0.$$

In the displayed monomial basis of $R$, multiplication by
$x^{42}$ kills precisely the ideal $(x)$. Therefore

$$f\in(x). \tag{783.7}$$

We also need the missing linear coefficient to vanish.
R779 proves the actual Kummer invariance of

$$
n_R=Qc+\frac{11}{2}u,\qquad
Q=-i x^{40}z^{42}+x^{41}z^{41}+i x^{42}z^{40}.
\tag{783.8}
$$

The pure-$p$ term vanishes in (783.5).
Write $f=c_{10}x+\text{terms of total degree at least two}$,
using (783.7).
The degree-$83$ part of $Qf$ is

$$c_{10}(-i x^{41}z^{42}+x^{42}z^{41}).$$

Its $z$-multiple is $c_{10}x^{42}z^{42}$.
Since the actual class is $S$-invariant and the top monomial is
nonzero in $R$, $c_{10}=0$.

Consequently the actual expansion has the form

$$
\boxed{
f=A x^2+B xz+O(I^3),\qquad I=(x,z).
}
\tag{783.9}
$$

There is no $z^2$ term because of (783.7).
The notation $O(I^3)$ means an actual element of the ideal
$I^3$ in the finite group ring, not an analytic approximation.

## 3. Independence of the auxiliary scalar norm preimage

Replace $v_q$ by another actual local norm preimage $v'_q$
of the same $w_q$, keeping $u_s$ fixed.
In the original basis there are unique $a(s),d(s)\in\mathcal O[S]$
with

$$v'_q=a(s)v_q+d(s)u_s.$$

Apply $N_S$ and use the independence of $u,w_q$.
The two exact augmentations are

$$a(1)=1,\qquad d(1)=0.$$

The local group ring $\mathcal O[S]$ has maximal ideal $(p,s-1)$,
so $a(s)$ is a unit. In the quotient (783.5) the new coordinate
of $c$ is $f'=a(s)^{-1}f$.
Modulo $p$, $a(s)^{-1}=1+O(z)$.
Because $f\in I^2$, this proves

$$f'\equiv f\pmod{I^3}.$$

Hence both $A$ and $B$ in (783.9) are independent of the
norm preimage. No unidentified determinant or scalar unit is
allowed to change their normalization: the equation
$N_Sv_q=w_q$ fixes the augmentation exactly to one.

The Gamma branch is the already fixed R704 branch.
Even translating that branch by a power of $\gamma$ would
multiply $f$ by a group-ring unit congruent to one modulo $I$,
and would leave this quadratic term unchanged. We retain
the original branch anyway and make no new choice of $\gamma$.

## 4. The quadratic coefficient is the exact original obstruction scalar

Multiply (783.9) by $Q$ in $R$. The two surviving products are

$$
(-i x^{40}z^{42})(A x^2)
  + (x^{41}z^{41})(B xz)
  =(B-iA)x^{42}z^{42}.
$$

Every monomial of $QI^3$ has total degree at least $85$.
The largest total degree of a nonzero monomial of $R$ is $84$,
so $QI^3=0$. Thus the equality is exact:

$$
Qf=\Theta N_\Gamma N_S,\qquad \Theta=B-iA.
\tag{783.10}
$$

By (783.4), the image of the actual $n_R$ in the quotient
therefore equals $\Theta w_q$ modulo $p$.
The localization of R779's original descended class is

$$
(\mathfrak d_R)_p
=A_R(d_q)_p+B_R(d_0)_p,\qquad A_R=9b/4.
$$

The $d_0$ term lies in the known pure-$p$ line generated by $u$,
and the $d_q$ term is represented by $w_q$ by (783.2).
The quotient is free, and restriction to the scalar extension
is injective on these Kummer classes by R779 §6.
It follows that

$$\Theta=A_R=9b/4=13b.$$

This proof does not assume $b\ne0$.
In $\mathbf F_{43}$ the inverse of $13$ is $10$ and
$17\cdot10=-2$. Substituting the already proved
$\Omega_2/\varepsilon=22+17b$ gives exactly (783.1).
In particular $\Theta\in\mathbf F_{43}$ even though the
individual group-ring coefficients initially lie in $k$.

Neither $A$ nor $B$ has been computed in this argument.
The statement that an original coefficient is canonical is
not an evaluation of that coefficient.

## 5. Exact local freedom while preserving both Euler norms and reality

The following statement explains why solving the norm equations
alone cannot provide the missing Robert input.

Let $h_0\in U$ satisfy $\tau h_0=h_0$, and set

$$
\delta c=xz\gamma^{21}h_0,\qquad c'=c+\delta c.
\tag{783.11}
$$

Then, in the integral local-unit module,

$$
N_\Gamma c'=N_\Gamma c,\qquad N_Sc'=N_Sc,\qquad
\tau c'=-c'.
\tag{783.12}
$$

The two norm equalities follow from $N_\Gamma x=N_Sz=0$.
For the last equality, the actual involution satisfies

$$
\tau x\tau=-\gamma^{-1}x,\qquad \tau z\tau=z.
$$

Consequently

$$
\tau(xz\gamma^{21}h_0)
=-xz\gamma^{-22}h_0=-xz\gamma^{21}h_0,
$$

where the last equality is the exact order-$43$ relation.
The original $c$ satisfies $\tau c=-c$ by R779 §2, proving
(783.12).

Modulo $p$, multiplication in the same original group ring gives

$$
Qxz\gamma^{21}=x^{42}z^{42}=N_\Gamma N_S.
$$

Hence the corresponding actual local Kummer vector changes by

$$
\boxed{n_R(c')-n_R(c)=N_\Gamma N_Sh_0.}
\tag{783.13}
$$

This change can be any vector in the original real
two-dimensional local Kummer space.
Indeed $N_\Gamma$ is onto because the Gamma semilocal algebra
is split, and $N_S$ is onto in the nontrivial character by
R701. Given a real target, first lift it through the two
norms and then average the lift with its $\tau$ conjugate.
Division by two is integral and $\tau$ commutes with the
combined norm, so the real target is unchanged.
R779's injective local restriction and the fixed character
descent identify these vectors with the original
$b_1,b_{45}$ space.

Thus even the exact Euler norms, the real law, and the
correct R704 embeddings do not determine the output of a
generic local unit supplied to (783.8).
The displayed perturbation changes the actual input, rather
than merely changing its coordinate basis.
This is why it does not contradict §3.

## 6. The genuine global ordinary lattice is a different constraint

No $h_0$ in §5 has been asserted to be a global ordinary unit.
That omission cannot be filled by a local norm-surjectivity
argument or by weak approximation.

There is a precise distinction when $b\ne0$.
R773 proves that the full actual global ordinary lattice
$E_V$ is generated over the commuting group rings by
$c_s,u_s,y$, with

$$
N_\Gamma c_s=(1-s^{11})u_s,\quad
N_Su_s=u,\quad N_\Gamma y=u,\quad sy=y.
$$

Taking the combined norm on these actual generators gives

$$
N_\Gamma N_Sc_s=0,\qquad
N_\Gamma N_Su_s=pu,\qquad
N_\Gamma N_Sy=pu.
$$

Since $u$ is primitive, this proves the exact global image

$$
\boxed{N_\Gamma N_SE_V=p\mathcal O u\qquad(b\ne0).}
\tag{783.14}
$$

In particular, if the $h_0$ in (783.11) is a genuine global
ordinary unit under this nonzero-$b$ hypothesis,
(783.13) is zero modulo $p$.
The local perturbations which change $\Theta$ therefore cannot
be realized by such global ordinary corrections.

This global assertion uses R773 only with its stated
$b\ne0$ hypothesis. It is not extended to $b=0$.
The unconditional scalar formula (783.1) and the local
freedom in §5 do not require that hypothesis.

## 7. Remaining actual arithmetic and scope

A successful computation may now produce the single canonical
quantity $B-iA$ from the original mixed Robert unit.
It need not store the full local coordinate matrix merely to
report this scalar. It must nevertheless prove that its
input is the same original $c_s$ and that its output uses
the norm normalization in §1.

At present the repository provides the original product
definition, its exact norm relations, and the R704 field
embedding matrix. It does not provide a fast evaluation of
the partial $q$-torsion product or of (783.9).
The original $q$ has 776 bits, and the raw product in
R311/R613 has $(q^2-1)/43$ $q$-direction factors.
No such enumeration is proposed as a runnable Actions job.

R779's $\varpi_s^{1938}$ input precision remains a sufficient
finite route when actual local unit values are available.
An alternative must establish an identity for the actual
quadratic coefficient of that same Robert product.
A Coleman power-series existence theorem or an arbitrary
norm-one solution is not such an evaluation.

This result supplies a canonical smaller arithmetic target
and a rigorous boundary for norm-based shortcuts.
It does not compute $b$, $\Omega_2$, the original first moment,
or a counterexample to Serre uniformity.
