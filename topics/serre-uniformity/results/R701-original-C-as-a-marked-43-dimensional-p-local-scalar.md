# R701: the original C is one marked scalar in a 43-dimensional p-local quotient

**Status: proved exact arithmetic reduction; the scalar has not been
evaluated.** This result keeps the same original $p=43$, $q$, $\gamma$,
$s$, $P_+$, Robert units, and ordinary class $C$ as R561. It reduces
the original $C$ to a specific finite $p$-local linear calculation in
the scalar field $F_s$, without an unknown global norm unit $y$ in
the input vector. It does not assign a value to $C$, $m$, or $b$.

## 1. Exact statement and original markings

Use

$$
\mathcal O=\mathbf Z_{43}[i],\quad k=\mathcal O/43,
\quad\Gamma=\langle\gamma\rangle=C_p,
\quad S=\langle s\rangle=C_p,
\quad\pi=\gamma-1,
\quad a=15i/7.
$$

The fields are $F$, $L=FL_q$, $F_s=B^T$, and $M_s=LF_s$.
The exact units are the original $u_s$, $c_s=N_Tc_B$,
$c_q=N_Sc_s$, and $y$. Their relations are

$$
N_Su_s=u,\quad N_\Gamma y=u,\quad
N_\Gamma c_s=(1-s^{11})u_s,\quad N_\Gamma c_q=0,
$$
$$
N_Sc_s=c_q,\quad
py=u+aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}c_q,\quad
\pi y=a\gamma^{22}c_q.
\tag{701.1}
$$

Let

$$
D_S=\sum_{j=0}^{42}js^j,
\qquad H_{11}(s)=\sum_{j=0}^{10}s^j,
\qquad \alpha_F=1+89460y_F.
$$

The marked prime of $F$ satisfies
$\operatorname{div}(P_+[\alpha_F])=P_+\mathfrak q_0$.
The unit coefficient projector is still the unaveraged
$P_+=44e_{\chi^-}$.

For a field $V$, write $U_V$ for the character summand of its
principal $p$-local units, taking all primes over $p$ if necessary.
The extension $L/F$ is split at $p$, so

$$U_{M_s}\simeq\mathcal O[\Gamma]\otimes U_{F_s}.$$

Define the input unit expression

$$
\boxed{
V_{\rm sc}
=\pi^{41}D_Sc_s-11a\pi^{40}c_q
\in E_{M_s}.
}
\tag{701.2}
$$

Its reduction in $U_{M_s}/p$ is $\Gamma$-invariant and therefore
descends uniquely to an element $\overline V_{\rm sc}$ of
$U_{F_s}/p$. Define the actual finite local quotient

$$
\mathscr Q_s=U_{F_s}/(pU_{F_s}+E_{F_s}).
\tag{701.3}
$$

The full pure-$p$ unit lattice is
$E_{F_s}=\mathcal O[S]u_s$, and

$$\boxed{\mathscr Q_s\simeq k[S].}$$

The image

$$\xi_\alpha=[P_+[\alpha_F]]_p\in\mathscr Q_s$$

is nonzero and spans the $S$-socle of this module. The exact
original arithmetic identity is

$$
\boxed{
[\overline V_{\rm sc}]
=\frac{C}{a}\,\xi_\alpha
\quad\text{in }\mathscr Q_s.
}
\tag{701.4}
$$

Thus define $\eta_p$ as the **unique coefficient** in (701.4).
It is the original $C/a$, not an arbitrary scalar from a ray
module. Since $a\equiv-4i\pmod{43}$ and $C\in i\mathbf F_{43}$,

$$
\boxed{
\eta_p\in\mathbf F_{43},\qquad
C=-4i\eta_p,\qquad
\mathcal L=31-2\eta_p.
}
\tag{701.5}
$$

The original relation $\Omega_2=\varepsilon(10-\mathcal L)$
consequently becomes
$\Omega_2=\varepsilon(2\eta_p-21)$ with the same $\varepsilon$.
No value of $\eta_p$ is supplied here.

## 2. The full local quotient really has dimension 43

R327 proves $U_F\simeq\mathcal O^2$ and that the same primitive
$u$ generates a direct summand $E_F=\mathcal O u$.
The scalar extension $F_s/F$ is cyclic of degree $p$, totally
ramified at $p$, with $\Delta$ centralizing its local Galois group.
Local reciprocity makes the norm cokernel trivial in the
nontrivial character, hence $N_SU_{F_s}=U_F$.

Lift a basis of $U_F$ through this norm. The resulting map
$\mathcal O[S]^2\to U_{F_s}$ is an isomorphism: modulo $p$
every nonzero kernel would meet the socle generated by $N_S$,
but the two norm images remain independent modulo $p$ in the
upper unit lattice. The invariant lattice is $p$-saturated
because the upper local unit lattice is torsion-free. Equal
ranks and Nakayama complete this integral argument.

Similarly $N_Su_s=u$ and primitivity imply the full ordinary
lattice $E_{F_s}=\mathcal O[S]u_s$, by the same one-generator
socle argument used in R657/R686. In the free local lattice
$U_{F_s}\simeq\mathcal O[S]^2$, the localization of $u_s$
has primitive augmentation. One coordinate is therefore a
unit in the local ring $\mathcal O[S]$. It is a unimodular
column, proving the full integral quotient

$$U_{F_s}/E_{F_s}\simeq\mathcal O[S].
\tag{701.6}
$$

This proves (701.3)'s dimension $43$ over $k$, without assuming
an unknown regulator at the scalar layer.

R377 proves that the original $q$ Frobenius is primitive in
the rank-one $p$-ramified $\chi^-$ block of $F$. In the
local-unit description this is exactly the primitivity of
$[P_+[\alpha_F]]$ in $U_F/E_F$, up to the fixed minus sign
of positive global reciprocity. The present statement uses
the **positive local element $P_+[\alpha_F]$**, not its
negative Frobenius convention, so there is no sign choice in
$\xi_\alpha$.

Choose a local norm preimage in $U_{F_s}$ of this local element.
Its image in the free quotient (701.6) has primitive
augmentation and is therefore a basis. Extension of the
original local element to $F_s$ is the $S$ norm of this
preimage, hence generates the invariant submodule. Modulo
$p$ it is the nonzero socle $kN_S$. This proves the assertion
about $\xi_\alpha$.

## 3. A simultaneous field Hilbert-90 normalization

The original ordinary scalar cocycle is

$$v_s=u_s-y+aD_\Gamma^{\rm bal}c_s.$$

R561 defines $C$ by choosing a field element $z_s$ with
$(s-1)z_s=v_s$, descending its full invariant divisor to a
divisor $D_s$ of $L$, and setting $[D_s]=Ce_q$ in $A_L$.
All outside-support terms are part of this definition.

Set

$$V_s=a\gamma^{22}D_Sc_s+aH_{11}(s)u_s,
\qquad W_s=V_s-11a y.$$

The balanced derivative and the exact Euler factor in (701.1)
give

$$
\pi v_s=(s-1)V_s,\qquad
N_\Gamma V_s=11a u,\qquad N_\Gamma W_s=0.
\tag{701.7}
$$

Indeed $(\gamma-1)D_\Gamma^{\rm bal}=p\gamma^{22}-N_\Gamma$,
$(s-1)D_S=p-N_S$, and
$D_S(1-s^{11})=-H_{11}(s)(p-N_S)$.

For an arbitrary original $z_s$, the difference
$\pi z_s-V_s$ is $S$-invariant and therefore lies in $L$.
Its Gamma norm is $-11a u$. Adding $11a y$ makes that norm
zero, so field Hilbert 90 over $L/F$ gives an element $r\in L$
with

$$\pi r=\pi z_s-V_s+11a y.$$

Replace $z_s$ by $z=z_s-r$. This changes $D_s$ by a principal
divisor of $L$ and does not change $C$. It gives the simultaneous
equalities

$$\boxed{(s-1)z=v_s,\qquad\pi z=W_s.}
\tag{701.8}
$$

This normalization is only a **field** Hilbert-90 step. The
element $z$ is not asserted to be an ordinary unit.

## 4. The descended divisor and its exact q coefficient

Both right sides of (701.8) are ordinary units. Thus the full
divisor of $z$ is fixed by both $S$ and $\Gamma$. As in R561,
write

$$\operatorname{div}_{M_s}z=j_{M_s/L}D.$$

The divisor $D$ is Gamma-invariant and has class $Ce_q$.
The only ramification of $L/F$ is at $q$ in this character;
the $p$ and $2$ divisor summands are zero. Every other invariant
divisor descends integrally. Consequently there are a full
divisor $D_0$ of $F$ and $d\in\mathcal O$ such that

$$D=j_{L/F}D_0+dP_+\mathfrak Q_0.$$

Since $A_F=0$ and $e_q$ has order $p$,

$$d\bmod p=C.$$
\tag{701.9}

The coefficient $d$ records the full ordinary class and cannot
be replaced by a ray coefficient. Norming the divisor gives

$$N_\Gamma D=pD_0+dP_+\mathfrak q_0.$$

On the other hand, the $S$ equation in (701.8) implies

$$H=N_\Gamma z-D_Su_s\in(F^\times\otimes\mathcal O)_{\chi^-},$$

and norm compatibility of full divisors gives
$\operatorname{div}_F H=N_\Gamma D$.
Choose $h_0$ with $\operatorname{div}h_0=D_0$, possible since
$A_F=0$. There is then an ordinary unit $e\in E_F$ with

$$H=ph_0+dP_+[\alpha_F]+e.
\tag{701.10}
$$

All character valuations at the $p$ prime vanish, so each term
of (701.10) defines a local principal-unit element after the
usual pro-$p$ projection. In $\mathscr Q_s$, the first term
vanishes modulo $p$ and the last is an ordinary unit. Equations
(701.9)--(701.10) prove

$$[N_\Gamma z]_p=C\xi_\alpha\quad\text{in }\mathscr Q_s,
\tag{701.11}$$

because $D_Su_s$ is also in $E_{F_s}$.

## 5. Elimination of z and y from the input vector

The same exact calculation as R695 gives

$$
\pi W_s=a\gamma^{22}
       (\pi D_Sc_s-11a c_q).
$$

Using (701.8) and multiplying by $\pi^{40}$ gives

$$V_{\rm sc}=a^{-1}\gamma^{-22}\pi^{42}z.$$

The characteristic-$p$ group-ring identity is
$N_\Gamma\equiv(\gamma-1)^{42}\pmod p$.
At $p$, $z$ is a local unit in the indicated character even
if its full global divisor is nonzero. Therefore

$$
V_{\rm sc}\equiv a^{-1}N_\Gamma z
       \pmod{pU_{M_s}}.
\tag{701.12}
$$

The right side is Gamma-invariant. Since $U_{M_s}$ is free
over $\mathcal O[\Gamma]$, reduction identifies
$U_{M_s}^\Gamma/p$ with $(U_{M_s}/p)^\Gamma$; this proves
the unique descent in Section 1. Combining (701.11) and
(701.12) gives the exact identity (701.4).

The proof used $y$ and $z$ only to certify this identity.
The test vector (701.2) itself contains neither unknown.

## 6. Relation with R695 and the Kummer test

In R695's full field $M'=LB$, put

$$Q_\kappa=\pi D_Sc_s-\kappa\pi D_Tc_t-11a c_q.$$

The same simultaneous normalization for the two $P=S\times T$
cocycles, of classes $C$ and $9$, gives the more general identity

$$
\boxed{
[\pi^{40}Q_\kappa]_p
=a^{-1}(C-9\bar\kappa)
 [P_+[\alpha_F]]_p
\quad\text{in }U_B/(pU_B+E_B).
}
\tag{701.13}
$$

The proof is identical: the simultaneous field primitive has
fully invariant divisor with ramified $q$ coefficient
$C-9\bar\kappa$, and its Gamma norm differs from
$D_Su_s-\kappa D_Tu_t$ by the principal norm in $F$.
In particular the calibrated coefficient of the anti term is

$$
[\pi^{41}D_Tc_t]_p
=\frac9a[P_+[\alpha_F]]_p
=13i[P_+[\alpha_F]]_p.
\tag{701.14}
$$

This is a genuine identity between the original $p$-local
Robert derivative and the marked principal $q$ element.
It uses the actual ordinary $C_t=9$ only as the second
cocycle's coefficient; it does not set $C=9$.

For completeness, the relation with integral division is also
exact. In a torsion-free ordinary unit lattice $E$ with
$E^\Gamma=E_B$, the quotient $E/E_B$ is $p$-torsion-free
and is an $R_\Gamma$ module. Since $p$ is a unit multiple
of $\pi^{42}$ in that DVR, for $Q\in\ker N_\Gamma$ one has

$$Q\in\pi^2 E
\Longleftrightarrow\pi^{40}Q\in pE+E_B.$$

In the reverse implication, lift the equality from $E/E_B$;
the remaining difference is in $E_B$ and has Gamma norm
zero, so it is zero by torsion-freeness. R700 proves $E_{M'}$
is $p$-saturated in $U_{M'}$, since $U_{M'}/E_{M'}$ embeds
in the torsion-free $X_{M'}^{(p)}$. Consequently

$$
Q_\kappa\in\pi^2E_{M'}
\Longleftrightarrow
\pi^{40}Q_\kappa\in pU_{M'}+E_B.
$$

Formula (701.13) makes the unique residue class
$\bar\kappa=C/9$ explicit. Direct local $\pi^2$ division
without the quotient by the **known global** $E_B$ would
pass for every $\kappa$ and is not this test.

## 7. A concrete finite certificate, still to be computed

The scalar form (701.4) requires an $86\times44$ linear
certificate over $k$, not an unknown full unit basis of $M'$.
In any exactly certified basis of $U_{F_s}/p$:

1. Form the $86\times43$ matrix whose columns are the actual
   local vectors of $s^j u_s$, $0\le j\le42$.
2. Append the actual vector of $P_+[\alpha_F]$. The proved
   rank of this augmented matrix is $44$.
3. Compute the actual vector of (701.2), using the same
   global Robert products at the $43$ Gamma-labelled $p$
   completions, and descend it by (701.12).
4. Solve for its unique coefficient $\eta_p$ in the last
   column. Verify the complete vector equality, not just a
   selected coordinate, and verify $\eta_p\in\mathbf F_{43}$.

The image is proved to lie in this $44$-dimensional span.
Failure of that equality would detect an incorrect local model,
a missing phase, an incorrect Robert tuple, or a coordinate
error; it must not be repaired by choosing a convenient root.

A finite local precision suffices. At $p$ the scalar field
has absolute ramification index $e=44\cdot43=1892$.
For a uniformizer $\varpi_s$, logarithm and exponential are
inverse on $U^{46}$, since $46>1892/(43-1)$. Hence

$$U^{1938}=(U^{46})^{43}\subset (U^1)^{43}.$$

Thus local unit values modulo $\varpi_s^{1938}$ determine
their full classes modulo $43$rd powers. A valid computation
must also certify the actual local field and its basis,
the character projection, and the images of the lower
filtration levels; simply taking a truncated logarithm before
its convergence threshold is insufficient.

The missing numerical input is now precise: the original
Gamma-labelled $p$-local values of $c_s$ and $c_q$ (and the
corresponding local Robert $u_s$ basis) at this certified
precision. They have not been evaluated in this result.
No Actions request, coordinate interface, or proof of finite
precision is counted as the original scalar computation.
