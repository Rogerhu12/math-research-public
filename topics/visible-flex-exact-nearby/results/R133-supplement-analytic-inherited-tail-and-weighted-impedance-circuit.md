## WIC4. Positive-pivot impedance elimination

The following calculation applies to any rho_i>0, so in particular to (WIC.13). Write
$$
 a_i=\frac{h_i k\widehat\eta_i q_i^2}{2\rho_i},\qquad
 b_i=\frac{h_i k\widehat\eta_i}{2\rho_i}.
 \tag{WIC.17}
$$
These letters b_i are halfstep coefficients, not the independent outer coordinate b_j. Starting from p_N=p0, define backwards
$$
 \begin{split}
 d_i^{\rm piv}&=1+2b_ip_{i+1}+a_ib_i,\\
 p_i&=\frac{(1+a_ib_i)p_{i+1}+2a_i}{d_i^{\rm piv}},
 &\tau_i&=\frac{1-a_ib_i}{d_i^{\rm piv}},\\
 \lambda_i&=\frac{2b_i(p_{i+1}+a_i)}{d_i^{\rm piv}},
 &\mu_i&=\frac{2a_i(1+b_ip_{i+1})}{d_i^{\rm piv}},\\
 \sigma_i&=\frac{2b_i}{d_i^{\rm piv}},
 &\upsilon_i&=\frac{2a_ib_i}{d_i^{\rm piv}} .
 \end{split}
 \tag{WIC.18}
$$
The outer elimination gives g_N=g^o+p0 g^e. Put
$$
        g_i=\tau_i g_{i+1}-\lambda_i r_i^o-\mu_i r_i^e .
 \tag{WIC.19}
$$
Then zeta_0=0 and the forward recurrence is
$$
 \zeta_{i+1}=\tau_i\zeta_i+\sigma_i(g_{i+1}+r_i^o)
                              -\upsilon_i r_i^e,\qquad
 \xi_i=g_i-p_i\zeta_i .
 \tag{WIC.20}
$$
Return d_j=xi_0, b_j=zeta_N-g^e and the full v_i from (WIC.14). Direct substitution proves every original row in (WIC.14), in both directions.

Since a_i=b_iq_i^2,
$$
 \begin{split}
 p_i-p&=\frac{2b_i(q_i^2-p^2)}{d_i^{\rm piv}},\\
 p_i-q_i&=\frac{(p-q_i)(1-b_iq_i)^2}{d_i^{\rm piv}},
 \qquad p=p_{i+1}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{WIC.21}
$$
Thus p_i lies between p and q_i. It is positive, and d_i^piv>=1. Also
$$
 d_i^{\rm piv}+(1-a_ib_i)=2(1+b_ip),\qquad
 d_i^{\rm piv}-(1-a_ib_i)=2b_i(p+a_i),
 \tag{WIC.22}
$$
so |tau_i|<=1. No inverse of Id-h_iD_i/2 is used. Its possible singularity is irrelevant to the positive pivot in (WIC.18).

When eta_hat_i=0, all halfstep coefficients vanish, p_i=p_{i+1}, tau_i=1, and (WIC.14) is the correct identity cell. There is no root curvature denominator.
