$$

Its diagonal entries are convex combinations of positive eigenvalues of $r(hD)$. Thus it is entrywise nonnegative with positive diagonal and positive determinant, giving positive impedance pivots. Unrestricted matrix Padé positivity is not claimed.

One sufficient explicit choice is $|ha|,|he|\le1$: the old parameter fact (15) puts the scaled spectrum in $(-3,3)\subset(-\sqrt{12},\sqrt{12})$. Both propagation rates must then be paid separately. The normalizer is generally a two-by-two matrix, so the scalar-normalizer code above is not copied to that case without its complete matrix change.

The primary candidate in WVC6--13 is tracefree. It retains the stronger scalar normalizer, nonincreasing propagation in both directions and unconditional positive-pivot result, without a new spectral gate.
