## 4. The conservative signed-square binding

For a real interval ball $X$, define

$$
\operatorname{square}(X)=X\cdot X.
\tag{R97R.1}
$$

For every real $x\in X$, the pair $(x,x)$ belongs to $X\times X$, hence $x^2\in X\cdot X$. Thus (R97R.1) is a rigorous enclosing realization of the same scalar square. It may be wider than an optimized square, and this continuation does not clip a negative lower endpoint or assert a sharper square.
