## CY2. The constant principal ratio is exactly unitary

Define
$$
                      \mathcal C_\mu=D_+D_-^{-1}.
 \tag{CY.6}
$$
For real $\mu$, $D_-^{-1}$ is a contraction on every $H^s$. On a nonzero signed physical frequency $n=4j$,
$$
 (\mathcal C_\mu)_j
   =\frac{1-i\mu/(2n)}{1+i\mu/(2n)},\qquad
 (\mathcal C_\mu)_0=1.
 \tag{CY.7}
$$
Consequently $\mathcal C_\mu$ is unitary on every $H^s$ and commutes with $I$, real translations and the phase Fourier projections. The exact identity
$$
                  \mathcal C_\mu-1=\mu I D_-^{-1}
 \tag{CY.8}
$$
includes the zero mode, where both sides vanish.

The complete individual principal ratio is
$$
 \mathcal A=T_oT_i^{-1}
 =\mathcal C_\mu+
        (M_{v_o}I-\mathcal C_\mu M_{v_i}I)T_i^{-1}.
 \tag{CY.9}
$$
Thus
$$
 \|\mathcal A\|_{L^2\to L^2}
 \le 1+\frac{K_{i,0}}4
                   \big(\|v_i\|_\infty+\|v_o\|_\infty\big).
 \tag{CY.10}
$$
The corresponding negative-window bound is
$$
 \|\mathcal A\|_{H^{-4/3}\to H^{-4/3}}
 \le 1+\frac{K_{i,-}}4
     \big(\|v_i\|_{\mathcal W_{4/3}}
                     +\|v_o\|_{\mathcal W_{4/3}}\big).
 \tag{CY.11}
$$
Both follow directly from the complete primitive norm $1/4$ and the unitarity of $\mathcal C_\mu$. They do not contain a separate $|\mu|$ factor. No inverse of a compressed principal block was used to prove them.
