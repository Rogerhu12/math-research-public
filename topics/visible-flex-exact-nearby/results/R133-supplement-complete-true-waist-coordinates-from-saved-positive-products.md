## 3. Three full angular derivatives of every shorter flow

For any trigonometric polynomial $f$ supported in $|j|\le B$,

$$

 \le (4B)^k\sqrt{2B+1}\,\|f\|_2

 \tag{WX.10}
$$
Apply this to $f=d_\phi$, $B=192$. In particular
$$
 \|d_{\phi\phi}\|_\infty\le\kappa_2D,\qquad
 \|d_{\phi\phi\phi}\|_\infty\le\kappa_3D,\qquad
 \kappa_2=768\sqrt{385},\quad
 \kappa_3=768^2\sqrt{385}.
 \tag{WX.11}
$$
This finite-support inequality makes no numerical Fourier summation.

Write $J=\chi'$, $K=\chi''$, $L_3=\chi'''$ for either directed flow, with initial values $1,0,0$. Along the corresponding time orientation, the variational equations are
$$
 \begin{split}
 \dot J&=\pm d_\phi J,\\
 \dot K&=\pm d_\phi K\ \pm d_{\phi\phi}J^2,\\

       \ \pm3d_{\phi\phi}JK\ \pm d_{\phi\phi\phi}J^3 .
 \end{split}\tag{WX.12}
$$
The signs depend on the chosen orientation and do not affect the absolute bounds.
If $A(t)=\int_0^tD$, then $e^{-A}\le J\le e^A$.

$|K|\le\kappa_2e^A(e^A-1)$.
For $L_3/J$, the complete integrand is bounded by
$3\kappa_2^2D e^A(e^A-1)+\kappa_3D e^{2A}$.

$$
