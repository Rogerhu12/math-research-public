with its genuine passive lift. Its curvature component is $v_\nu$,

APHI's phase functional only uses the original intrinsic selected

[APHI](R147-supplement-actual-nonzero-phase-border-of-the-strict-parameter-particular.md),
[ASB](R147-supplement-actual-short-parameter-errors-and-refined-signed-borders.md)

c_H&\in(-.02547,-.02513),\qquad |c_H^{-1}|<40,\\
c_A&\in(-.006275,-.006264).
\end{split}                                                   \tag{HP.1}
$$

Here $c_P$ is the original principal phase scalar, $c_H$ is the

## HP2. Exact elimination with the entire regular defect

Use the raw linearization and signs of
[PP.2--5](R147-supplement-actual-phase-pivot-with-full-regular-defect-retained.md).
Separate just the physical mean-height row from its complete
regular vector. Denote the remaining vector, including the entire
nonmean raw phase equation, by

$$
\mathcal A_\circ u+g_\circ\kappa=f_\circ .
$$

Here $u$ includes the curvature, conjugacy, passive frame/position
and all original independent endpoint variables. Write the two
separate scalar equations as

$$
\mathcal H u+d_H\kappa=\eta,\qquad
\ell u+\omega\kappa=\sigma .                            \tag{HP.3}
$$

The symbol $\eta$ in (HP.3) is a prescribed linear height
right-hand side, not the fixed intrinsic design parameter
$\eta_0$. The full original height differential is $\mathcal H$;
its independent world and geometric ports are part of that
functional. In the original PP convention

$$
\ell u=\langle Lh+(E'/J)a\rangle,\qquad
\omega=\langle b_\nu\rangle ,
$$

and $\mathcal A_\circ$ retains
$P_0[Lh+a-a\circ R+(E'/J)a]$. No raw $E'a/J$ term is
silently removed at a different base point.

Put

$$
u=z+\kappa Z_\nu,\qquad
r_\circ=\mathcal A_\circ Z_\nu+g_\circ .
                                                               \tag{HP.4}
$$

The scalar entries after this shift are
$\mathcal H Z_\nu+d_H=c_H$ and
$\ell Z_\nu+\omega=c_P$. Hence the original complete system is
equivalent to

$$
\boxed{\begin{split}
(\mathcal A_\circ-r_\circ c_H^{-1}\mathcal H)z
   &=f_\circ-r_\circ c_H^{-1}\eta,\\
(\ell-\rho_P\mathcal H)z&=\sigma-\rho_P\eta,\\
\kappa&=c_H^{-1}(\eta-\mathcal H z),\qquad
u=z+\kappa Z_\nu .
\end{split}}                                                   \tag{HP.5}
$$

Conversely (HP.5) reconstructs every equation in (HP.3--4),
including the original height equation. Thus neither a regular
inverse nor a finite determinant is assumed in this reduction.

Only the full body and original state-jump entries already solved
by NP vanish in $r_\circ$. The nonmean phase function, waist,
physical fourth Robin, independent mean and physical-coordinate
entries are all retained. A purely local exterior trace does have
zero variation for the precisely frozen exterior in HP1; that
fact does not delete a nonlocal exterior-to-waist functional or a
variation of its input chart.

For an original output row $\mathcal R_i u+d_i\kappa$, its actual
reduced row is exactly

$$
\mathcal R_i-\frac{\mathcal R_iZ_\nu+d_i}{c_H}\mathcal H .
                                                               \tag{HP.6}
$$

All original direct derivatives $d_i$ must occur. In particular
the parameter-dependent chart terms $B\partial_\nu\Xi$ and
$\ell\partial_\nu\Xi$ from RNS.24--26, and the derivative of a
state-dependent left normalization acting on the nonzero base
residual, remain as in PP4. Physical response bounds below do
not absorb an unidentified direct row or chart term.

The height-first system (HP.5) and PP's phase-first system are
two exact eliminations of the same original equations. Neither
claims that the response obtained after regular correction is
still $Z_\nu$, or that its corrected scalar Schur entry equals
one of (HP.1).

## HP3. Actual phase receiver after the height elimination

Use the original source quantities of
[PRN.3](R149-supplement-complete-phase-receiver-in-original-negative-source-windows.md):

$$
\begin{split}
D_*&=\|h(r_*)\|_{H^{-2/3}\oplus H^{-1}},\\
F_-&=\|f\|_{L^2([r_c,r_*];H^{-2/3}\oplus H^{-1})},\\
F_{\rm st}&=\|f\|_{L^2([0,r_c];H^{-1}\oplus H^{-1})},\\
J_-&=\sum_{r_a\in[r_c,r_*)}\|j_a\|_{H^{-2/3}\oplus H^{-1}},\\
J_{\rm st}&=\sum_{r_a\in(0,r_c)}\|j_a\|_{H^{-1}\oplus H^{-1}} .
\end{split}                                                   \tag{HP.7}
$$

Here $r_c=2333968743003/10^{13}$ and $r_*$ is the original
true root. A jump at $r_c$ is assigned to $J_-$ once. Both
state means, all Fourier modes and every original impulse remain;
the displayed radial norms use ordinary $dr$. These are the same
quantities received by CW.3.

Let $L_\Omega h$ be PRN's whole distributed intrinsic phase

$$
\mathcal P_Hh=L_\Omega h-\rho_P\mathcal L_bh .

PRN.1, CW.3 and (HP.2), without an angular norm conversion, give

$$
\boxed{\begin{split}

148568(D_*+J_-)+10512F_-\\
&+4736F_{\rm st}+13989J_{\rm st}.
\end{split}}                                                   \tag{HP.9}
$$

For example its exact outward pre-rounding coefficient of
$D_*+J_-$ is $2600+1.543(94600)=148567.8$.
The other three are $10511.299$, $4735.032$ and

The full height differential also contains the actual port

and any separately prescribed propagated geometric impulses.

The original absolute frame has determinant $-1$, whereas

More explicitly, decompose the full remaining scalar row as

$$
\ell u=L_\Omega h+P_{\rm ph}(u),\qquad

$$

Here $P_{\rm ph}$ retains the raw conjugacy/chart terms, and

Their sufficient scalar costs are respectively

For a single true varying vertical translation $Y_b=t e_z$,
$K_b=0$, the exact height port is $t$, since $\Pi J=1$.
Thus (HP.12) has the exact coefficient $-\rho_P t$.
The translation has not disappeared through the fixed-base

introduced. General frame, position and mean ports remain

For a continuous TC trial corrected by a true solution, its

## HP4. The paid finite physical column and its independent means

Let $F=F_\nu^{\rm phys}$ be precisely PO/ASB's original
physical even half-sum density response. The whole ASB bounds
recalled in PP.7 imply

$$
\begin{split}
\|F/c_H\|_2&<.063856,&
\|P_{|j|\ge97}F/c_H\|_2&<.002121,\\
\|B^{-1}F/c_H\|_2&<6.749,&
\|P_{|j|>2^{4608}}B^{-1}F/c_H\|_2&<.002121 .
\end{split}                                                   \tag{HP.13}
$$

The operator is exactly
$B=\mathrm{Id}+(\Pi_+M_w\Pi_++\Pi_-M_w\Pi_-)I$.
Its intermediate normalized modes $97\le|j|\le2^{4608}$
are retained. Neither the fourth bound nor the raw physical
high97 bound is a cutoff97 estimate for $B^{-1}F$.
No Theta coordinate or full incoming normalization is inferred.

Let $s\in\mathbb R^{96}$ consist of the original whole
uninflated signed NP physical trial balls used in PP6, with their
original phases, signs and orthonormal
$\sqrt2\cos(4j\theta)$ tests. ASB.10 pays

$$
\|\mathcal O_{96}F-s\|_{\ell^2}<E:=6\,10^{-7},
\qquad \|s\|_{\ell^2}<.0016047+E .                    \tag{HP.14}
$$

Choose only as a finite computational normalization
$h_0=-.0253$. From (HP.1), $|c_H-h_0|<.00017$, so

$$
\begin{split}
\left\|\frac{\mathcal O_{96}F}{c_H}-\frac{s}{h_0}\right\|_{\ell^2}
&\le \frac{E}{.02513}
 +\frac{.00017(.0016047+E)}{.02513(.0253)}\\
&<.0004532 .
\end{split}                                                   \tag{HP.15}
$$

The whole signed balls are divided by a negative scalar, with
the interval endpoints reversed. Selecting their decimal centers
would additionally require all their original serialization and
quadrature radii. No such replacement is made.

For the independent physical density means $\mu_+,\mu_-$ of
this same true particular,
[ANP3's complete saved enclosures](R147-supplement-actual-independent-strict-nu-particular-and-signed-column.md)
give each whole ball $[+/-\,2.84\,10^{-5}]$.
These are two actual mean columns; neither is zeroed by the
half-sum projection or by reflection. Therefore

$$
\left|\frac{\mu_\pm}{c_H}\right|<.001131,\qquad
\left\|\frac{(\mu_+,\mu_-)}{c_H}\right\|_{\ell^2}<.001599 .
                                                               \tag{HP.16}
$$

The joint comparison squares the two complete mean bounds and
uses the same positive denominator $.02513$; it does not
subtract an old mean error upper from another upper. In
particular, for the true original physical98 vector

$$
V_H=\frac{(\mathcal O_{96}F,\mu_+,\mu_-)}{c_H}
$$

the combined consequences of (HP.13--16) are

$$
\boxed{\quad
\|V_H\|_{\ell^2}<.063877,\qquad
\left\|V_H-(s/h_0,0,0)\right\|_{\ell^2}<.001663 .
\quad}                                                        \tag{HP.17}
$$

The two zeros in this particular finite approximation are
approximation coordinates with the entire mean error (HP.16),
not claims that the true two means vanish. This is a complete
signed physical finite/mean approximation before a subsequent
incoming coordinate map, not a96-dimensional square inverse.
Any independent direct derivative in a residual mean row still
enters through (HP.6).
