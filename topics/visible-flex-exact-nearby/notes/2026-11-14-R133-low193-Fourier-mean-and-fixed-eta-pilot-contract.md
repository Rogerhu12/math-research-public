## 2. Keep the original exact mean as a varying scalar

Write $D=\partial_r-A$ for the full moving linearized operator at the
old centre, $q_{\rm tr}$ for the full physical trial and
$e_z$ for the already certified exact mean correction. Then

$$
q_*=q_{\rm tr}+e_z e_2,\qquad
R_{\rm non}=\Pi_{\rm non}(Dq_{\rm tr}-F_\nu),\qquad
C_{\rm non}=\Pi_{\rm non}Ae_2 .
$$

The exact old mean equation pays the independent even-mean row. Its
remaining nonmean source is

$$
R_{\rm non}-e_z C_{\rm non}. \tag{D.1}
$$

The derivative $e_z'$ belongs only to the independent even-mean row.
It must not be inserted a second time into (D.1). The new repaired
centre still has its complete additional mean-containing source fee;
the old mean equation does not zero that new fee.

The old completed body consumer bounds (D.1) by the sum of two
separately certified norms. The first projection pilot instead formed
a new sample-space expression after the nonmean Fourier projection.
That operation can widen coefficient intervals because Fourier boxes
have already been transformed back to independent sample boxes. This
is a loss of enclosure sharpness, not an invalidation of that pilot.

## 3. Direct Fourier linear combination

On one actual closed radial cell write

$$
R_{\rm non}=R_{\rm pol}+r_R,\quad
C_{\rm non}=C_{\rm pol}+r_C,\quad
\|r_R\|_{A^0}\le E_R,\quad
\|r_C\|_{A^0}\le E_C,\quad
\|C_{\rm pol}\|_{A^0}\le N_C .
$$

Choose and save an exact binary rational $e_0$ and certify the whole
cell bound $|e_z-e_0|\le\epsilon$. The new finite polynomial is defined
directly by its existing directed Fourier coefficient boxes:

$$
P=R_{\rm pol}-e_0C_{\rm pol}. \tag{D.2}
$$

No inverse-transform/sample-operation/forward-transform cycle is
used to define (D.2). Padding the radial coefficient lists with exact
zeros leaves the same shared radial variable. The complete uniform
remainder is

$$
E=E_R+|e_0|E_C+\epsilon(N_C+E_C). \tag{D.3}
$$

Indeed the exact omitted term is
$r_R-e_0r_C-(e_z-e_0)(C_{\rm pol}+r_C)$. Thus (D.3)
covers all scalar dependence without assuming that $e_z$ is constant.
All these errors retain a proved finite angular support.

The low projection onto the 193 physical slots is a coordinate
projection in Fourier space. It contracts $A^0$, so the projected
polynomial retains the full applicable (D.3). The output stores all
96 odd amplitudes, the independent even mean, all 96 nonzero even
amplitudes, and both uniform component remainders. The mode convention
is $-2\operatorname{Im}\hat h_j$ for sine and
$2\operatorname{Re}\hat h_j$ for cosine, with the mean undoubled.

For each scalar norm there is also the independent valid triangle
bound

$$
\|\Pi_{\rm low}R_{\rm non}\|
+\sup|e_z|\,\|\Pi_{\rm low}C_{\rm non}\|. \tag{D.4}
$$

The consumer may retain the smaller of the direct-polynomial norm
and (D.4). It records both. That smaller norm is **never substituted
for the uniform remainder attached to the saved direction polynomial**.
A later directional Green consumer must use the actual polynomial
and its own complete remainders, or separately use the scalar bound.
