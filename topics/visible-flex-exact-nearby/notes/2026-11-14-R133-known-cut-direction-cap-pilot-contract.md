## 5. Full Hessian bridge and actual trace

Let $M_H,A_H$ be the recorded complete physical-to-moving Hessian
multiplier and additive fee. The same estimate on a fixed vector is

$$
\epsilon_{\rm mov}(w_0)
\le M_H\epsilon_{\rm phys}(w_0)+A_H\|w_0\|_2. \tag{K.5}

$$
\epsilon_{\rm mov}(w)
\le\epsilon_{\rm mov}(w_0)
+\epsilon_{\rm mov}^{\rm old}\Delta_w. \tag{K.6}
$$

same true cap and matrix trial. It is not a claim that the full
