## New complete signed consumer

For a complete cell of length ell, let C and F be the original
interval enclosures of c and f. In the exact variation-of-constants
formula, the endpoint multiplier belongs to exp(ell C), and every
subsegment multiplier belongs to
$
 \mathcal E=\exp([0,\ell]C).
$
It is strictly positive. Therefore a valid endpoint source enclosure
is ell*E*F; a whole-cell source enclosure is [0,ell]*E*F. Thus
$
 \begin{split}
 D_{\rm right}&\in \exp(\ell C)D_{\rm left}
                       +\ell\mathcal E F,\\
 D_{\rm whole}&\in \mathcal E D_{\rm left}
                       +[0,\ell]\mathcal E F .
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{FR.5}
$
Here interval multiplication and addition are directed 320-bit Arb
operations. The signed F is used; it is not replaced by its absolute
value, its L2 norm, or an old scalar source gain.

Separately propagate G, B and A, starting from 1,0,0. The same endpoint
formula gives G'=cG, B'=cB+f, and A'=cA with all (FR.4) atoms added
to A. Intersect the direct D enclosure after each cell with
G*D(0)+B+A. Both enclosures contain the same actual D, so this
intersection is valid. The final report includes the full signed
propagated initial, bulk and interface contributions, their
complete endpoint enclosure and the minimum Y lower over all cells.

This consumer deliberately uses the existing complete native rows

## Physical and 1/10000 gates

On a whole cell whose computed Y enclosure is positive, m_flat is
the negative branch. The exact mean difference is
$
 m_{\rm flat}-m
 =\frac{-mD}{\sqrt Y(\sqrt n+\sqrt Y)}.             \tag{FR.6}
$
Since the 32-mode raw v has nonmean square
V=< (v-m)^2 >, full-circle Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$
 \|v\|_\infty\le -m+\sqrt{64V},\qquad
 \|v_{\rm flat}-v\|_\infty
 \le(-m+\sqrt{64V})
      \frac{|D|}{\sqrt Y(\sqrt n+\sqrt Y)}.          \tag{FR.7}
$
The raw u is unchanged. These estimates include all raw Fourier modes,
the exact physical mean, every radial cell and every original affine
parameter. They are not sample or collocation checks.

- true: the relevant whole-domain assertion has a directed proof;
- false: a saved directed witness proves failure for every parameter

- unknown: the current complete input ranges do not decide the
