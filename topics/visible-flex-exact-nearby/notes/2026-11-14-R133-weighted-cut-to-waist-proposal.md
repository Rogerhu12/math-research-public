## A continuous nonsingular radial weight

The orthonormal physical moving coordinate is `z=sqrt(W) h`, with
`W=diag(1/2 I_96,1,1/2 I_96)`. On the full common base partition choose
the positive dyadic nodal values recorded in the actual artifact, and
let `rho` be their exact continuous piecewise linear interpolation. The
sampling prescription is a floating approximation to
`(K^2+(1/64)^2)^(1/4)`; that approximation does not assert any exact
identity between `rho` and the true curvature.

The new coordinate is `y=R z`, where `R=diag(I_96,rho I_97)`. On each
open base cell its complete generator is

$$
A_R=R A R^{-1}+R'R^{-1},
\qquad (R'R^{-1})_{\rm even}=(\rho'/\rho)I_{97}.
$$

Thus the even mean is transformed and differentiated along with every
other even state. The weight stays positive at the curvature root.
Its exact continuity creates no artificial state jump. Any later
ordinary-source and boundary estimate must use the same `R`; true state
jumps become `R j`, and every projector jump still requires its actual
full-frame bound.

## Complete finite proposal and saved bytes

`2026-11-14-R133-complete-moving-cap-cut-boundary-v2-native.json.gz`.
The source changes it to the weighted coordinate and uses every column.
Within an actual common cell it constructs a degree-24 local fundamental
polynomial from a quadratic floating operator model, proceeds backward,

observed full-matrix step norm exceeds two. These floating samples and
step selection are diagnostics, not continuous enclosures.
