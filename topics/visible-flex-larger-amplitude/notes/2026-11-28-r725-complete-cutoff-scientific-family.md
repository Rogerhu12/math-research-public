## 3. Actual cutoff law and model identity

For the exact fixed epsilon, the implemented normalized law is

$
 C_\epsilon(t)=
 \begin{cases}
 \epsilon G(t/\epsilon)/(1-\epsilon),&0\leq t\leq\epsilon,\\
 (t-\epsilon/2)/(1-\epsilon),&\epsilon\leq t\leq1-\epsilon,\\
 1-\epsilon G((1-t)/\epsilon)/(1-\epsilon),&1-\epsilon\leq t\leq1,
 \end{cases}
$

where $G(u)=\tfrac52u^4-3u^5+u^6$, with constant extensions outside the unit interval. The exact definition records its value and first three normalized-coordinate derivatives at all four cuts, the orientation of each physical layer and all eight new internal physical cuts. It records C3 matching and explicitly does not claim C4 matching.

The original `_S5`, `_S5_prime`, raw-defect lift, bubble/gamma definitions and paired central profile bodies are unchanged. This implementation does not globally replace the symbol S5. The new boundary helper is selected only in the left and right boundary cases of `_cutoff_and_profile`. The right layer uses the reflected cutoff coordinate and its actual odd derivative orientation.
