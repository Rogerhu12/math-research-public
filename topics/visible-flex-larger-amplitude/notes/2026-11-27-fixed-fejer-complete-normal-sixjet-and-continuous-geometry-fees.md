## 8. R717: a same-leaf full-position norm bound

For a complete physical leaf, let F=(F_x,F_y,F_z) be its outward interval vector. The producer computes

$
 r_{\mathrm{leaf}}=\operatorname{upper\_sqrt}(F_x^2+F_y^2+F_z^2).
$

with the existing exact outward interval arithmetic. The three coordinate boxes are from the same entire leaf and angle cell. This is a valid upper bound for the Euclidean norm of every point in that leaf. It need not be sharp, and no numerical improvement is presumed.

The new leaf field is full_position_norm_upper. It is a rational value in memory and a rational string in the persistent stream. Statistics.add passes it through the original F(value) conversion; metadata and absorb retain it in the complete maxima dictionary. Thus the existing downstream full-stream reader can reconstruct the same statistic without a new field query.

Let U be the maximum of this field over every complete upper leaf. Let

$
 C=\operatorname{upper\_sqrt}\!\left(\max(R_+,1/5)^2+(|H|+1/1000)^2\right),
 \qquad D_{\mathrm{norm}}=2\max(U,C).
$

The maximum with 1/5 preserves the parent's formal full mark-support union even before a sufficient cap collar is proved. Once the actual cap radius contains the fixed supports, the usual cap radius R_upper applies. Both fixed cap disks and all original p4 graph pieces are covered.

Rotations about the fixed origin and lower reflection preserve Euclidean norm. Therefore every upper point, reflected lower point, rotated activity point and fixed cap point lies in the origin-centered ball of radius max(U,C). The triangle inequality covers every pair in this formal union, including activity-cap and two different phase-piece pairs.

The producer also retains its original complete coordinate-cylinder bound D_cylinder. It returns

$
 D_{\mathrm{formal}}=\min(D_{\mathrm{norm}},D_{\mathrm{cylinder}}).
$

Both candidates are independently valid upper bounds for the same formal complete union. Their minimum is valid and cannot weaken the old bound. No new root, integrand, observation angle or field evaluation is introduced.

The existing output key formal_reflection_fixed_caps_all_rotation_set_diameter_upper is preserved. Its definition now also records U, C, D_norm, D_cylinder, the selected minimum, the R717 reference and the complete formal-union scope. It does not populate full_marked_surface_diameter_upper or complete_closed_surface_reach_lower by itself. A source-specific proof of the closed physical family is still required before promoting this formal bound to the actual full-phase surface.
