## 3. Unique fixed Fejér chart and exact rounding

$$

$$

$$
\rho_0(\phi)=1+\sum_{m=1}^{160}(1-4m^2)c_m\cos(2m\phi),
$$

$$
\Theta_0(\phi)=\phi+\sum_{m=1}^{160}
\frac{(1-4m^2)c_m}{2m}\sin(2m\phi).

$$

$$
\rho_0(\phi)\ge\frac14-553\,2^{-53}>0.
