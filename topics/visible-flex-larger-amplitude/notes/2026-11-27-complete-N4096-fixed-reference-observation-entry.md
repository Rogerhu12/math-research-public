## 2. Complete current angular carrier and exact positive weights

Let the actual original quarter words be $p_j$, $0\leq j\leq512$. They are first read from the actual unchanged reference problem and checked against the original binary64 formula. The new array is constructed by

$$
\widetilde p_{2j}=p_j,
\qquad
\widetilde p_{2j+1}=\operatorname{fl}\!\left(\frac{\pi(2j+1)}{2048}\right).
$$

The even assignment copies the old array words. It does not calculate a new formula and infer that its values ought to agree. The helper verifies the complete even subsequence by bytes and records every old and new hexadecimal word. No angle cap, subsampling, or low-frequency assumption is used.

Current quarter multiplicities are 2 at the two endpoints and 4 at each of the 1023 interior columns. Their sum is 4096. The even subsequence has exactly the original multiplicities and total mass 2048. The odd subsequence consists of 512 entries of multiplicity 4 and also has total mass 2048. Thus, for any fixed fully evaluated field and the same radial weights and scalar loss,

$$
\Phi_{4096}=\tfrac12\Phi_{\rm even}+\tfrac12\Phi_{\rm odd}
$$

in exact arithmetic. The even measure uses the complete old material angles, evaluated on the **current** field. It is not a saved old parent statistic. The maximum over the complete current grid contains the maximum over its even subset. No conclusion about the intervening continuum follows from these finite identities.

Both standard-library validation and runner construction bind the complete lists of angle words, array dtype/shape/bytes hashes, multiplicities, source hash, reference N, current N, and definition hash. The output arrays are made read-only. Runtime source hashes are part of the model before its identity is computed.

The SO3 field is nonpolynomial in angle. The implementation does not infer a finite angular bandlimit from M160, and does not treat a rule such as N greater than a multiple of M as an accuracy certificate.

## 11. Combination with M320 and remaining radial work

This delivered source is deliberately M160. The published M320 implementation already separates fixed reference M160/frequencies from current M320/frequencies and supplies the exact three-channel zero embedding. To combine it with this observer, a separate complete source family must retain that M split, use the N helper against the original reference problem, broaden only the explicitly supported current-M model contract, and update the complete parent/layout/typed/reader identities. Radial refinement remains a distinct implementation obligation. Existing arbitrary-point provider queries use original reference-cell/u addresses. A new physical radial mesh cannot feed fine cell indices directly to that provider, or round a fine affine node into an old binary64 u and call it the same exact point. It needs a complete exact-affine endpoint representation, for example $(\alpha,\beta)$ with $s=\alpha S+\beta$, and a source-bound exact conversion for every original and new node. It also needs complete actual old-quintic restriction, all old/new radial observation transfer, an explicit new radial integration/calibration rule and complete root re-reading on that rule. The present N-only source does none of these operations and does not claim otherwise.

R710/R713/R715 and subsequent conditional fee components are not automatically valid under a new source/model identity. They may be reused only through complete explicit consumers for the actual current literal angles, seed batches, integration and working values. More angular observations do not pay the continuous angular bound, normal-root error, inverse/rounding error, or full marked-reach certificates.
