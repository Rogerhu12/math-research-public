# R777 supplement. An opposite-cap material path excludes both retained-cap branches

Use the original R764 initial metric $g_0$ and R777 physical material labels, including the complete pre-roll of arclength $E=.44$, standard roll of arclength $L=.132\pi/2+.0005$, $T=E+L$, and central half-width $B=.88$. Let $s_m=0$ modulo the full section perimeter be the original leftmost wall equator, whose old section position in the common raw frame is $(x_m,0)$ and whose outward section normal is $(-1,0)$. The R774 §5 original half-wall integral gives $x_m<-1.454$. Choose one material point on each signed cap, both at $(s,w)=(s_m,0)$ in the true terminal cap-normal chart. Their zero-phase coordinates have $(x,z)=(x_m+d_b,0)$, with $.132\le d_b<.1325$; the R760 graph height above its terminal plane is exactly zero there. The initial curve at fixed $s_m$ crossing one full signed roll, the full unmarked central axial generator, and the other full signed roll joins this pair. Both meridians have unit speed, and the axial part has length $2B$. Thus this one explicit initial material path has length $2(B+T)$.

Work at the last prescribed phase $\theta=.90$ before the final common grip rotation, which preserves distances. Both R777 poses use $\cos\delta=d/\sqrt{d^2+\Delta^2}>0$ and $\sin\delta=\Delta/\sqrt{d^2+\Delta^2}$, where $d=X_{R,0}-d_b-.30<.519$ and $\Delta=Y(z_r)-Y(z_l)=z_l-z_r>.312$ by R777 §2. Thus $\sin\delta>.515$. The pair-posed field has $b=.105$. In the balanced field $b=.10+\log(1+\exp(60(P_y-.10)))/60>.10$; the final start gate is one. Their planar translations and original raw-body translation affect only the $x,z$ components at a fixed cap label.

For either pose the outward physical transverse coordinate at the chosen terminal material point is exactly
$$
    y_{\rm out}=B+b-\sin\delta\,(x_m+d_b-.30)
                         +\cos\delta\,[Y(0)-Y(z_l)] .
$$
Here $Y$ is the actual even bending primitive, nonincreasing in positive $z$, so $Y(0)-Y(z_l)\ge0$. Moreover $.30-x_m-d_b>1.6215$. The two full retained caps are reflections in physical $y$, with otherwise identical $x,z$ at the chosen common label, so their Euclidean distance is $2y_{\rm out}$. Hence
$$
 d_{\rm end}>2(B+.10+1.6215\cdot.515)>2(B+T).
$$
Any exact $C^1$ isometric continuation of the middle on the initial tensor would send the specified initial path to a curve of the same length, shorter than this endpoint distance. There is also a **uniform positive lower bound on the relative metric error** under these two fixed terminal point positions. If the largest absolute eigenvalue of $g_0^{-1/2}(F^*I-g_0)g_0^{-1/2}$ is at most $\epsilon$ on the path, its length is at most $2\sqrt{1+\epsilon}(B+T)$. It follows that
$$
 \epsilon\ge[d_{\rm end}/(2(B+T))]^2-1
       > [(B+.10+1.6215\cdot.515)/(B+T)]^2-1>.40 .
$$
For the last strict inequality $B+T<1.529$ and $B+.10+1.6215\cdot.515>1.814$. This bound concerns the continuous path; a finite angular/transverse grid is not guaranteed to realize its supremum.
