## 2. A single physical immersion of each full strip

$$

$$
For each $|y|\le B$, the implicit coordinate is an increasing bijection $[0,l]\to[0,l]$ with derivative $D=1+y\eta'>0$ and the original endpoints fixed. This is an entire ruled surface, not merely a prescription on the two long edges. The identities $(B_f+\eta T)'=\eta'T$, $r_S=1/D$, $r_y=-\eta/D$ give
$$

Thus $F^*\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle=dS^2+dy^2$ everywhere, including both axial boundaries, and the absolute principal curvatures are $0$ and $|\kappa|(1+\eta^2)/D$. $$

Here $[w]_\times h=w\times h$. When both controls have their initial values this solution is exactly the identity rotation and a constant incoming translation. In a terminal interval where the controls equal their initial ones the outgoing relative rotation is constant and the map is a rigid image of the original strip. Requiring $R(l)=I$ and $\gamma(l)=q^0(l)+d_{\rm out}$ therefore matches the actual neighboring whole panel through every available derivative. In particular, matching the two ends under these six conditions moves the grip and the lower panel by their prescribed *different* rigid motions while giving a single closed, periodic cylinder.

## 3. Genuine local existence for the prescribed two-ended displacements
