We give a strongly \(C^2\)-continuous family of closed \(C^2\) embeddings on \(0\le t\le\pi/2\). Both complete disks develop isometrically in the same four physical directions. The right disk fixes its centered radius-\(.18\) grip pointwise and moves every rim point except four at all positive phases. It is an explicit thick starting field for the same fixed metric.

We use \(0\le\xi\le.02\), including the definite choice \(\xi=.02\). The reference \(F_t\) has transverse component \(rn\) and axial coordinate \(A\). Thus \(r_0=1/22.70\) is a common lower bound for the original global reach.

## One diffeomorphism of physical space

Let \(h=a/\sqrt2\). List the signed outward directions as \(+X,-X,+Z,-Z\). Choose amplitudes \(0<b_j\le b=.025\), including the actual \((.020,.022,.024,.025)\) in that order. For a signed scalar coordinate \(u\) and phase \(t\), use the R781 clipped clock \(P_c\) with its constant extensions:
\[
 \beta_j(u,t)=b_j\sin^2t\,P_c((u-h)/.08),\qquad
 D_j(u,t)=\int_h^u(\cos\beta_j-1),\qquad H_j(u,t)=\int_h^u\sin\beta_j .
\]
The integrands vanish below \(h\), and \(|\partial_u\beta_j|\le B=b/(.08\cdot .94)=b/.0752\). Suppress \(t\) in the notation. For \(i=X,Z\) set
\[
 C_i(u)=u+D_{+i}(u)-D_{-i}(-u),\qquad
 H_i^*(u)=H_{+i}(u)+H_{-i}(-u).
\]
Apply one map to **every** point of the physical reference, including the full central cylinder, both rolls, and both disks:
\[
 {\cal S}_t(X,Y,Z)=(C_X(X),\ Y+H_X^*(X)+H_Z^*(Z),\ C_Z(Z)),\qquad G_t={\cal S}_t\circ F_t.
\]
Each \(C_i'\ge\cos b>0\), and its two tails have constant positive slope. Inverting the two scalar \(C_i\)'s and then subtracting the two heights gives a global \(C^2\) inverse. The time dependence is strong \(C^2\) on bounded sets. At \(t=0\) the map is the identity, so \(G_0=F_0\) pointwise with the original \(g_0\). The same composition on the original nine charts preserves all four physical seam two-jets and regularity at the polar centers. Explicitly for either local material coordinate \(i,j\), \(G_i=D{\cal S}(F)F_i\) and \(G_{ij}=D{\cal S}(F)F_{ij}+D^2{\cal S}(F)[F_i,F_j]\). ## The global signed normal-ball inequality after both simultaneous cap bends

Here all chords are taken in ordinary three-dimensional physical space. For two arbitrary points, order coordinates as \((X,Z,Y)\). Their image difference is the following secant matrix times their difference:
\[
 T=\begin{pmatrix}c_x&0&0\\0&c_z&0\\s_x&s_z&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For either horizontal axis, \((c_i,s_i)\) is an average, along the entire signed coordinate interval of that pair, of unit vectors of the form \((\cos\beta,\pm\sin\beta)\), including \((1,0)\) on the central interval. For a zero coordinate difference take the derivative at the coordinate. Thus \(c_i\ge\cos b,\ |s_i|\le b,\ c_i^2+s_i^2\le1\). The first two diagonal entries of \(T^\top T\) are in \([\cos^2 b,1]\), and the last is one. The off-diagonal horizontal-to-axial block has norm at most \(\sqrt2 b\); the remaining symmetric horizontal cross term has norm \(|s_xs_z|\le b^2\). Using \(\cos^2 b\ge1-b^2\) gives
\[
 m^2=1-\sqrt2 b-2b^2,\quad M^2=1+\sqrt2 b+b^2,\qquad
 m^2|q-p|^2\le|{\cal S}_t(q)-{\cal S}_t(p)|^2\le M^2|q-p|^2 .
\]
This applies even if the straight chord between the original endpoints exits the body. At a single point the first two columns are actually unit vectors; hence \(\|D{\cal S}^\top D{\cal S}-I\|\le\sqrt2 b+b^2\). The only nonzero ambient second partials are \(XX\) and \(ZZ\), each of norm at most \(B\), since differentiating the pertinent planar unit tangent has norm \(|\beta'_j|\). For arbitrary unit vectors \(v,w\), the inequality \(|v_Xw_X|+|v_Zw_Z|\le1\) gives the global bilinear bound \(\|D^2{\cal S}\|\le B\).

Fix any original sphere phase and any two distinct points \(p,q\) on it. Its reach \(r_0\) gives \(2|n_p\cdot(q-p)|\le |q-p|^2/r_0\) for either unit normal. In detail, the two tangent open balls of radius \(\tau<r_0\) on the two sides avoid the surface; square the two distances and let \(\tau\) increase to \(r_0\). If \(n'\) is a unit normal to the new surface at \({\cal S}(p)\), then \(D{\cal S}(p)^\top n'=\lambda n_p\) and \(|\lambda|\le M\). The integral Taylor remainder on the full ambient chord has length at most \(B|q-p|^2/2\). Consequently
\[
 \frac{2|n'\cdot({\cal S}(q)-{\cal S}(p))|}{|{\cal S}(q)-{\cal S}(p)|^2}
 \le\frac{22.70 M+B}{m^2}<24.36 .
\]
The final inequality follows, for example, from \(\sqrt2<1.415,\ M<1.018,\ B<.333,\ m^2>.96337\) at \(b=.025\). For any two nearest points at common distance \(d<1/24.36\) on the new compact closed embedded surface, their chord and the normal direction towards that point would attain the value \(1/d\) on the left. This contradiction gives \(\operatorname{reach}G_t\ge1/24.36\) for all phases, including all two-cap, cap–roll, opposite-head, and central point pairs. Finally \(24.36(.03)(1.362)<1\). In particular all phases clear the target reach relative to the **unchanged initial** diameter. The argument bounds nonlocal collisions as well as the curvature limit of near pairs.
