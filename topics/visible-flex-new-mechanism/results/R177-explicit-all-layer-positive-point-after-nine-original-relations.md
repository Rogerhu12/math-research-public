## 3. 静态与二次层的直接正证

$$
L_0=\frac14h^{-2}(1+k^2)^2\ge\frac14h^{-2}.             \tag{6}

$$

\left(1+\frac{k^2}{4}\right)(k')^2
-k^3v k'+(1+k^2+k^4)v^2
\right\}.
$$

$$
\boxed{\quad
