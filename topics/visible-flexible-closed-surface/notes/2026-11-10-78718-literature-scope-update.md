R. Sa Earp、E. Toubiana，*Discrete and nondiscrete isometric deformations of surfaces in R³*，Siberian Mathematical Journal 43:4 (2002), 714–718；俄文版 887–893。

https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=smj&option_lang=eng&paperid=1338&wshow=paper
